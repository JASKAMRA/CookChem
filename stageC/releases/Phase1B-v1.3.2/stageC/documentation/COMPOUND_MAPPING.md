# Compound mapping

Stage B `Compound_ID` is the stable reference. Supported identity signals are exact ID, exact canonical name, explicitly sourced synonym, PubChem CID, and full InChIKey. Contradictory signals or multiple candidates result in an ambiguous mapping. Similarity/fuzzy names do not verify chemical identity. Isomer-sensitive full keys are retained.

Mappings expose verified, ambiguous, unmatched, rejected, and pending identity-review outcomes. Stage B identity-pending entries cannot be labelled verified by automatic mapping. Manual decisions require a rationale and source. A mapping alone does not make a food concentration verified or eligible for exposure. The 19 Stage B identity reviews and 105 incomplete food-occurrence reviews remain unresolved inherited limitations; the 17 supplement/medicinal-botanical contexts are not automatically ordinary foods.
