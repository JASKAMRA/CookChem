# Batch 011 — polyphenols across fruit, vegetables and spices

Twenty-five distinct Stage B targets were screened against a multi-matrix primary study (16 foods; 18 target analytes; HPLC-PDA quantification, LC-ESI-QTOF/MS characterization). The screening file distinguishes measured exact analytes from source-panel limitations and identity conflicts.

Seven exact measurements were ingested from Table 2: gallic acid, chlorogenic acid, caffeic acid, ferulic acid, para-hydroxybenzoic acid, quercetin and kaempferol. Table values are mg/100 g fresh basis, mean ± SD, with three independent measurements. Food samples were edible portions purchased in Melbourne; cultivar was not specified. The earlier p-hydroxybenzoic candidate is resolved as para-hydroxybenzoic acid (Stage B 4-hydroxybenzoic acid).

Generic catechin/epicatechin rows were not mapped to stereospecific Stage B identities. Coumaric acid was not assumed to be the p-isomer. Epicatechin gallate was not confused with EGCG. Flavonoid glycosides were not converted to aglycones. No exposure was calculated.
