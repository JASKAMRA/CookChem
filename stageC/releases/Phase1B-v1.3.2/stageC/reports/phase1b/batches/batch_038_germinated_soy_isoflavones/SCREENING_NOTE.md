# Batch 038 — Exact isoflavones in germinated soybean

## Screening decision

Prepared 90 candidate food records from Table 4 of Chen et al. (2024): five exact analytes (daidzin, genistin, glycitein, daidzein, genistein) × 18 yellow/black soybean sprout conditions (0, 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2, 4, 8, 12, and 16 cm). The primary table reports means ± SD in nmol/g (n=5). Candidate rows retain these reported quantities and convert the mean to mg/kg using compound-specific molecular weights. The paper quantified each analyte separately by validated UPLC-DAD and used LC-QTOF-MS/MS to confirm identities; authentic standards are described for all five analytes.

## Source and methods

Chen H, Aili R, Wang M, Qiu F. “Transformation profiles of the isoflavones in germinated soybean based on UPLC–DAD quantification and LC–QTOF–MS/MS confirmation.” *Food Chemistry: X*. 2024;22:101413. DOI: 10.1016/j.fochx.2024.101413; PMID 38707783. Full text: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11068514/. Yellow and black soybeans were purchased from a Beijing supermarket, soaked at 25 °C and germinated in darkness with twice-daily rinsing; samples were collected at specified sprout lengths, dried at 60 °C and powdered. Five replicates were conducted for each sample. Dried powders were extracted by 70% methanol reflux for 2 h.

## Basis and limitations

Table 4 labels values as nmol/g and the paper says results were corrected using yields from Table 2. The candidate basis is conservatively recorded as dried germinated sample, but the exact yield correction/denominator should be adjudicated before acceptance. No fresh-weight values are inferred. Each row represents a germination condition, not an independent market lot; the five replicates and SD should not be interpreted as broad product variability. All rows remain pending Stage B mapping and checksum-gated ingestion.
