# Batch 032 — Chrysin in Greek honey matrices

## Screening decision

Prepared five honey-matrix candidate rows for chrysin using targeted UHPLC-q-TOF-MS results from Koulis et al. (2022). Table 1 reports medians (mg/kg honey): blossom 3.6 (n=62), fir 0.35 (n=10), oak 0.72 (n=24), pine 0.65 (n=39), and thyme 0.061 (n=34); chrysin LOD is 0.032 mg/kg. These are matrix medians, not individual sample records. Candidate rows do not count as accepted coverage.

## Source and method

Koulis GA et al. “Thorough Investigation of the Phenolic Profile of Reputable Greek Honey Varieties: Varietal Discrimination and Floral Markers Identification Using Liquid Chromatography–High-Resolution Mass Spectrometry.” *Molecules*. 2022;27(14):4444. DOI: 10.3390/molecules27144444. https://www.mdpi.com/1420-3049/27/14/4444. The paper analyzes 169 honeys collected from different Greek regions over 2016–2017. Samples were homogenized; 1 g honey was acidified, extracted three times with ethyl acetate, dried, evaporated, reconstituted and filtered. Table 1 gives targeted median concentrations.

## Review limits

Group summaries do not provide individual sample values or distribution details in Table 1, and pooled regions/years limit lot specificity. Check Stage B compound mapping and preserve median semantics before considering ingestion. Candidate remains outside the active dataset pending the Stage B checksum gate.
