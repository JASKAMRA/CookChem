# Batch 041 — Hesperidin in fresh clementine juice

## Screening decision

Prepared three candidates from Table 6 of Leporini et al. (2020), for clementine cv Comune juices from Cetraro, Rosarno and Corigliano Calabro, Calabria: 81.08 ± 4.94, 40.06 ± 3.04 and 65.30 ± 3.54 mg/100 mL, respectively. Values are mean ± SD from n=3 analytical replicates. Normalized values are converted to mg/L juice (×10); reported values and the volume basis are retained. No density conversion or fresh-weight inference was made.

## Source and method

Leporini M, Loizzo MR, Sicari V, Pellicanò TM, Reitano A, Dugay A, Deguin B, Tundis R. “Citrus × Clementina Hort. Juice Enriched with Its By-Products (Peels and Leaves): Chemical Composition, In Vitro Bioactivity, and Impact of Processing.” *Antioxidants*. 2020;9(4):298. DOI: 10.3390/antiox9040298; PMID: 32260119. Full text: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7222210/.

The paper lists authentic hesperidin among purchased standards. Juice fruit were washed, peeled and squeezed; the juice was centrifuged and filtered. HPLC-DAD used a C18 column with detection at 287 nm, and analyte identity was checked against standards by retention time and characteristic spectra. Table 6 reports the fresh juice marker values directly as mg/100 mL.

## Limitations

Triplicate values are analytical replicates for regional juice samples, not independent fruit-lot variance; 25 fruits were collected per region but individual values are not reported. The values apply to fresh-squeezed, pre-enrichment juice. Records remain candidates pending Stage B mapping review and checksum-gated ingestion.
