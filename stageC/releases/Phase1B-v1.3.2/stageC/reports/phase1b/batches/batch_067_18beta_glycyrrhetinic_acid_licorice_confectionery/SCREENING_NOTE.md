# Batch 067 — 18β-glycyrrhetic acid in licorice products

Full text and Table 5 verified in Sabbioni et al. (2006), *Phytochemical Analysis* 17:25–31, DOI 10.1002/pca.877, PMID 16454473, PMC7167644. The paper explicitly identifies compound 2 as 18β-glycyrrhetic acid and reports a validated isocratic HPLC assay that separates glycyrrhizin from its aglycone. Commercial licorice foods were extracted with water:ethanol 50:50, and authentic standards were quantified by HPLC-UV with selectivity/recovery validation.

Table 5 reports free 18β-glycyrrhetic acid (percent w/w): Sirea Oronero chips 0.13%, Sirea Oronero cylinders 0.04%, Tabu cylinders not detectable (<LOD), Saila cylinders 0.03%, Saila roots 0.25%, and Sirea roots not detectable. These correspond arithmetically to 1,300, 400, and 300 mg/kg for the three positive confectionery products, and 2,500 mg/kg for Saila roots; roots are retained as a distinct food ingredient context, not treated as confectionery. No zeros are inferred from n.d. The product analysis is a primary quantitative food-composition result.
