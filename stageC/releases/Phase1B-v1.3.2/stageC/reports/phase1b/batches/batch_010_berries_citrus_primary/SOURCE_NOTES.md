# Batch 010 — berry phenolics and grapefruit constituents

Twenty-five distinct Stage B compounds were screened against two relevant primary full texts and a targeted grapefruit-juice UPLC study. Outcomes are in `compound_screening.csv`; compound-level search events were appended to `SEARCH_LOG.csv`.

Accepted only exact named analytes in the berry paper Table 2 **Free** column; esterified and insoluble-bound fractions were not combined. Values retain µg/100 g dry weight. ND is not treated as zero. Catechin/epicatechin were not remapped because the paper does not specify the Stage B stereoisomer; p-hydroxybenzoic acid and epoxybergamottin remain held for identity review.

The furocoumarin study reports mean (SD), n=3, in ng/g. Grapefruit bergapten (0.7 ng/g) is below LOQ (2.54 ng/g), so it is excluded; grapefruit juice bergapten (30.2 ng/g) is above LOQ. Grapefruit values for DHB and bergamottin were accepted.

The grapefruit flavonoid paper reports µM values adjusted to juice; candidates remain pending because no juice density supports conversion to mass-per-food-mass. No exposure was computed.
