# Batch 045 — Herbacetin and isoquercetin in Canadian small fruits

Prepared 21 positive quantitative candidate rows from Kodikara et al. (2025), Tables 2a/2b: Herbacetin (7) and Isoquercetin (14). Table values are means ± SD in mg/kg dry weight, n=3. Herbacetin cells reported as <LOQ or NF are excluded. Isoquercetin is kept as the paper's exact analyte label and separate Stage B target; it is not merged with quercetin-3-galactoside.

Source: Kodikara C, Sura S, et al. “Bioactive Compounds of Indigenous Canadian Small Fruits: UHPLC-HRMS-Based Phytochemical Characterization, Mineral Composition and Their Antioxidant Activity.” *ACS Food Science & Technology*. 2025;5(12):4467–4487. DOI: 10.1021/acsfoodscitech.5c00610. https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/acsfoodscitech.5c00610

The study sampled ripe edible-stage Canadian prairie fruits near Winnipeg, freeze-dried and ground them with seeds, and reports dry-weight concentrations from UHPLC-HRMS. Review Table S2 for analyte-specific standards and isomer discrimination, Methods for extraction, and whether n=3 represents independent biological samples; only one composite per fruit type is described. Candidates are not accepted mappings and do not raise coverage. The Stage B checksum gate remains in effect.
