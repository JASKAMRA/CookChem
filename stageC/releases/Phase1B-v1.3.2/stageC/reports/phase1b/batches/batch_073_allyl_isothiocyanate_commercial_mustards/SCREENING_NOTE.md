# Batch 073 — allyl isothiocyanate in commercial prepared mustard

## Source and measurements

Eib et al. (2020), “Relationship between mustard pungency and allyl-isothiocyanate content: A comparison of sensory and chemical evaluations,” *Journal of Food Science* (DOI 10.1111/1750-3841.15383), quantified AITC in eight commercial mustard products (A–H). The source reports fresh-weight means ± SD (n=3 analytical replicates) and product moisture. An AITC reference standard (≥99%) and benzyl isothiocyanate internal standard were used with multiple-point HPLC-PDA calibration (R²=0.999). Values have been transcribed from Table 2 and converted from mg/100 g FW to mg/kg FW by ×10.

| Product | Label | AITC (mg/100 g FW; mean ± SD) | Moisture (%) |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| A | Sweet | 43.9 ± 3.5 | 50.1 |
| B | Sweet | 28.8 ± 0.7 | 44.6 |
| C | Medium piquant | 21.3 ± 0.4 | 71.6 |
| D | Medium hot | 38.1 ± 4.9 | 77.0 |
| E | Medium hot | 99.7 ± 1.7 | 56.7 |
| F | Hot | 135.6 ± 1.8 | 66.3 |
| G | Extra hot | 183.1 ± 1.9 | 66.6 |
| H | Not defined | 34.0 ± 0.7 | 70.3 |

The article states each product was extracted and analyzed in triplicate. The three determinations are analytical replicates for each commercial product, not three independent brands/lots. Product labels remain anonymized and seed species are not assigned to individual products.

## Screening decision

Eight exact-analyte candidate records prepared. Preserve the as-sold product and fresh-weight basis; no dry-weight conversion is used. Candidate rows await Stage B checksum-gated ingestion.
