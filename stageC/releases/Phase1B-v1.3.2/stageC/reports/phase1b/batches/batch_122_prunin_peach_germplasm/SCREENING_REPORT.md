# Batch 122 — Prunin in mature peach pulp

**Decision: accepted; one identity-complete compound added.** Coverage rises from 124/130 to **125/130**.

Zhang et al. profiled mature peach fruit from 217 accessions using LC-QTRAP-MS/MS. The paper identifies peak 53 as naringenin-7-O-glucoside (Prunin), MRM 433→271, against an authentic 98% Prunin standard. Table 2 footnote a confirms use of the compound’s own standard curve; the methods report external calibration. Results §3.3 identifies Rihua5 as the accession with the highest Prunin at **33.333 μg/g fresh weight** (equivalent to 33.333 mg/kg FW). Mature fruit was collected at commercial maturity; pulp was analyzed.

Ten fruits per accession were split into three biological repetitions. The cultivar maximum is recorded as a study-specific measured value and must not be generalized to peach species or market supply.

Primary source: Zhang X, et al. *Food Chemistry* 403 (2023), 134471. DOI [10.1016/j.foodchem.2022.134471](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.foodchem.2022.134471); [primary full text PDF](https://www.saas.sh.cn/Upload/lg/Content/Article/File/2023/03/01/202303011053408719.pdf).
