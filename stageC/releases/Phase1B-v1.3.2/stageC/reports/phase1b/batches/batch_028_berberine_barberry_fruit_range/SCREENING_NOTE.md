# Batch 028 — berberine in barberry fruit (range only)

## Evidence found

Miłek, Dżugan & Litwińczuk (2023), *Postępy Fitoterapii* 24(1):3–8, DOI 10.25121/PF.2023.24.1.3. The abstract reports berberine ranges in barberry fruits measured by HPTLC against berberine sulfate standard: *Berberis vulgaris* 1–5.5 mg/100 g, *B. koreana* 1–21 mg/100 g, and ornamental *B. thunbergii* varieties 78 to >300 mg/100 g. The sampled fresh fruit was frozen at −60°C and lyophilized before analysis, while one comparison *B. vulgaris* sample was purchased as dried fruit. The paper fragment does not clarify whether the summarized range is on fresh fruit, freeze-dried material, or mixed material basis, and sample-level values/table are not exposed in the accessible text.

A separate study of edible Patagonian *Berberis microphylla* reported berberine at 0.001% in fruit, mainly in seeds (PMID 25495577), but the accessible abstract does not provide the reporting basis or sample-level values.

## Disposition

No numeric food-composition rows prepared: reporting basis and source-resolved point values are unavailable. Do not treat a species-wide range as individual food records or convert it to wet-weight content. Gap register remains uncovered pending full table/basis evidence.

Sources: https://doi.org/10.25121/PF.2023.24.1.3 ; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25495577/ .
