# Batch 070 — Gentisic acid in avocado pulp

## Primary source

Di Stefano V, Avellone G, Bongiorno D, Indelicato S, Massenti R, Lo Bianco R. “Quantitative evaluation of the phenolic profile in fruits of six avocado (Persea americana) cultivars by ultra-high-performance liquid chromatography-heated electrospray-mass spectrometry.” *International Journal of Food Properties* 20(6):1302–1312 (2017). DOI: 10.1080/10942912.2016.1208225. [Publisher PDF](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/pdf/10.1080/10942912.2016.1208225).

Table 2 reports gentisic acid in avocado pulp from six cultivars (“Bacon,” “Fuerte,” “Hass,” “Pinkerton,” “Rincon,” “Orotawa”) at green (just harvested) and ripe (ready to consume) stages. Ten combinations have a numerical value; the two `nf` entries are omitted, not interpreted as zero. Values are mg analyte/kg fresh sample. The paper purchased an authentic gentisic acid standard and quantified methanolic pulp extracts by UHPLC-HESI-MS.

For each cultivar and ripening stage, pulp from three fruits was homogenized. Methods state 36 independent extracts (six cultivars × two stages × three extracts). The Table 2 concentration gives one value per cultivar-stage and does not present an SD or explain whether these are biological or extraction replicates. Hence the ten exact-source rows are candidates pending replicate/statistical-unit review. The source reports no acid hydrolysis, so these are direct methanolic pulp measurements rather than hydrolysis-released totals. The authors note gentisic acid increased during ripening and may be synthesized postharvest; retain the ripening stage and avoid treating values as a market-wide range.
