# Batch 049 — Paradisin A in Ray Red grapefruit juice

A primary study quantified paradisin A among seven grapefruit cultivars and pummelo. Its abstract reports the Ray Red juice value as 0.059 ± 0.001 µg/mL. Figure 4 states values are mean ± SD, average of 15 samples. The article identifies the analyte using isolated material, authentic standards, HPLC-PDA, retention time and standard spiking; calibration R² is 0.9998.

Fruit was harvested at the Texas A&M University-Kingsville Citrus Center in Weslaco, Texas (November 2005). Five pounds per variety were juiced with a home blender, stored at −80 °C, and 50 mL was extracted three times with ethyl acetate. The result remains on a juice-volume basis (0.059 mg/L); no density conversion was made. The method reports an LOQ of 79 ng, not a directly comparable juice concentration.

This is one cultivar/site/harvest observation, so it is a candidate rather than population coverage. Exact Stage B ID/CID/InChIKey were checked; formal review and checksum-gated ingestion remain pending.

Source: Girennavar et al. (2008), *European Food Research & Technology* 226:1269–1275, DOI 10.1007/s00217-007-0654-4. [Full text](https://www.rapamycin.news/uploads/short-url/w17HpUg4TgcJWiLZyyAe85zQxDI.pdf).
