# Batch 030 — ursolic and oleanolic acids in apple peel

Butkevičiūtė, Janulis & Kviklys (2022), *Plants* 11(9):1247, DOI 10.3390/plants11091247, PMID 35567248. The study examined Galaval (Gala clone) apples grown on 17 rootstocks in Babtai, Lithuania. HPLC-PDA quantified ursolic and oleanolic acids separately using their standards. Source-reported peel values: ursolic acid 3.25 ± 0.16 mg/g at Cepiland-Pajam®2 and 5.84 ± 0.29 mg/g at 62-396-B10®; oleanolic acid 0.55 ± 0.03 mg/g at Cepiland-Pajam®2 and 1.03 ± 0.05 mg/g at EM_02. Four candidate records capture only these documented extreme samples, not all 17.

Apple peel samples were prepared as lyophilizate. The article reports mg/g and describes preparation of apple lyophilizate; records retain this basis and do not convert to fresh apple weight. Concentrations were means of three consecutive HPLC test results ± SD; these are not independent fruit-lot replicates. The rows remain candidates pending Stage B gate and mapping review.

Full text: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9100339/ .
