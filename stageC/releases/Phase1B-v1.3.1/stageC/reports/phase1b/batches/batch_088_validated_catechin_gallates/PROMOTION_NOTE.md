# Batch 088 — validated tea catechin gallates

Promoted 5 measurements: one mature Shan Tuyet tea-leaf value for (−)-catechin gallate and four green-tea plucking-period values for (+)-gallocatechin gallate. The former is 3.3 ± 0.2 mg/g dry leaf (n=3 independent batches), measured by validated HPLC against a >98% authentic standard with analyte-specific calibration (R² > 0.999). The latter values are retained on the source's reported mg/g basis with mean/SD metadata and plucking period in row notes.

Stereospecific identities were checked against the Stage B CID/full InChIKey and independent FDA/PubChem records. Stage B was not edited. Values describe dried leaves/material, not a brewed serving; do not treat as tea-infusion concentrations. Official ingestion and mapping are included in the active Phase 1 validation.
