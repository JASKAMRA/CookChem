# Batch 123 — final recheck of uncovered identity-complete compounds

**Outcome:** the five remaining identity-complete compounds were re-screened. None had a newly verified accepted quantitative ordinary-food measurement, so accepted coverage remains **125/130**. This batch records a search decision only; it adds no food rows or mappings. The requested six-record increase cannot be reached from this registry snapshot: five identity-complete compounds are currently uncovered. A sixth would require a formally verified Stage B identity and a qualifying food measurement in a new Stage B/Stage C successor.

## Screening decisions

| Compound | Decision | Evidence reviewed |
|---|---|---|
| Oroxylin A (`MOL-V10-5320315`) | Hold | A validated UPLC-MS/MS primary study quantified 34 batches of crude *Scutellaria baicalensis* stem-leaf and reported Oroxylin A values. The accessible primary abstract identifies the material as herbal-medicine stem-leaf; the numerical table was not directly available to verify its sample-level entries or establish those particular batches as Huangqin tea ingredient. Separate primary *Oroxylum indicum* fruit work reports no Oroxylin A in young edible fruit and locates it in mature fruit/pod-coat extracts described as normally non-edible. No new value is ingested. |
| 7-Methoxyflavanone (`MOL-V10-2825024`) | Hold | Exact-CID searches found biotransformation/method literature and similar but structurally distinct methoxyflavanones; no exact-analyte ordinary-food concentration with a reported quantitative method was verified. |
| 5,4′-Dihydroxy-3,6,7,8,3′-pentamethoxyflavone (`MOL-V12-CID-100625`) | Hold | Citrus-peel literature found positional isomers and qualitative identification leads, but no calibrated concentration for the exact Stage B structure. The related citrus comparison source reported the target among compounds not detected; that does not provide a positive quantitative measurement. |
| Dodecyl gallate (`MOL-V15-CID-14425`) | Hold | The primary Hong Kong edible-oil paper is a validated method/measurement-uncertainty study, not accessible sample-specific occurrence data. The UK Food Standards Agency AO1020 report's gallate values are spikes, method-recovery trials, or prepared foods. A Turkish market survey screened 160 oils qualitatively and reported no gallates; it did not produce quantitative positive values. |
| Octyl gallate (`MOL-V15-CID-61253`) | Hold | The same occurrence/method review applies. A primary electrochemical paper tested commercial margarine, butter, and sunflower oil, but its table reports samples spiked with 0.30–0.90 μM OG and recovery; those are method validation values, not native food concentrations. The Turkish market survey reported no detected gallates by qualitative screening. |

## Source trail

- Yan et al. (2017), “Comparative analysis of 15 chemical constituents in *Scutellaria baicalensis* stem-leaf from different regions in China,” *Journal of Separation Science*, DOI [10.1002/jssc.201700473](https://doi.org/10.1002/jssc.201700473), PMID 28685933. The PubMed abstract confirms 35 batches and a validated UPLC-MS/MS method; no numeric table was accessible in the primary source during this pass.
- Rojsanga et al. (2023), *Molecules* 28:6837, [DOI 10.3390/molecules28196837](https://doi.org/10.3390/molecules28196837), full text [PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10574379/). The authors state Oroxylin A occurred in mature fruit and dry pod-coat extracts, normally non-edible parts; young-fruit extract had no detectable Oroxylin A.
- Sin et al. (2006), “Determination of five phenolic antioxidants in edible oils: Method validation and estimation of measurement uncertainty,” *Journal of Food Composition and Analysis*, DOI [10.1016/j.jfca.2005.12.005](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfca.2005.12.005). The accessible abstract describes a validated analytical protocol, not food sample result tables.
- UK Food Standards Agency, AO1020, [Development of methods for the analysis of antioxidants in a range of foods](https://www.food.gov.uk/sites/default/files/media/document/research-report-ao1020_0.pdf). Commercial sample tests in the report cover BHA; gallate numbers shown in the relevant sections are spike/recovery experiments.
- Bozok (1997), Ankara market survey, record [Tezara thesis 69342](https://tezara.org/theses/69342). The abstract reports qualitative oil screening; no gallates were observed, so no quantitative results were produced.
- Sivasankaran et al. (2016), “Nanomolar Level Determination of Octyl Gallate in Fats and Oils,” DOI [10.1007/s12161-015-0356-7](https://doi.org/10.1007/s12161-015-0356-7). Table 2 describes standard additions to commercial food samples and recovery, not unspiked native concentrations.
