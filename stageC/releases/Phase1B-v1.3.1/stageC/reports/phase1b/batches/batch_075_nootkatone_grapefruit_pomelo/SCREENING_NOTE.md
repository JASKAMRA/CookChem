# Batch 075 — nootkatone lead in grapefruit and pomelo peel

## Source and reported values

A 2019 review reproduces Ortuño et al. (1995), *Flavanone and Nootkatone Levels in Different Varieties of Grapefruit and Pummelo*, DOI 10.1021/jf00049a001, in Table 12. It reports immature-fruit sesquiterpene values of 5 ± 0.5 mg/100 g fresh peel for grapefruit and 0.9 ± 0.4 mg/100 g fresh peel for pomelo. The review lists “Nootkatone (Sesquiterpene)” in the analyte column and describes n-pentane extraction.

## Decision

Two candidate rows are recorded, held pending direct inspection of the primary table. The review’s result cells say only “Sesquiterpene,” so the exact numerical linkage to nootkatone is not sufficiently clear to accept. Sample counts and analyte-specific method details also need primary-source confirmation. Mature-fruit non-detects are not converted to zero or added as records. No accepted coverage changes.

Sources: [review Table 12](https://www.mdpi.com/2304-8158/8/11/523); [primary article](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/jf00049a001).
