# Batch 074 — tectochrysin in Greek honey

## Source and data

Koulis et al. (2022), “Thorough Investigation of the Phenolic Profile of Reputable Greek Honey Varieties: Varietal Discrimination and Floral Markers Identification Using Liquid Chromatography–High-Resolution Mass Spectrometry,” *Molecules* 27(14):4444 (DOI 10.3390/molecules27144444; PMID 35889316), analyzed 169 Greek honeys: blossom (n=62), fir (n=10), oak (n=24), pine (n=39), thyme (n=34). Tectochrysin was identified through suspect screening and semi-quantified against chrysin. Table 2 reports mean ± SD in mg/kg for positive matrices: blossom 0.049 ± 0.091, oak 0.030 ± 0.030, pine 0.024 ± 0.043. Fir and thyme are ND and are not represented as positive candidates.

## Screening decision

Three positive source-backed candidate rows prepared. They remain semi-quantified (no authentic tectochrysin calibration), aggregated by floral category, and held for review; they do not increase accepted coverage.

Source: [PMC full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9323402/), Table 2 and suspect-screening/calibration methods.
