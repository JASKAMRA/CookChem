# Batch 026 — beta-cryptoxanthin in orange and papaya

Holden et al. (1999), *Journal of Food Composition and Analysis* 12:169–196, DOI 10.1006/jfca.1999.0827. USDA/NCC Carotenoid Database Table 3 reports β-cryptoxanthin as a separate analyte in µg/100 g edible portion. Candidates: orange, raw, all commercial varieties 39 µg/100 g (3 sources, confidence B); papayas, raw 51 µg/100 g (1 source, confidence C). Converted to mg/kg fresh weight using ×0.01.

The database evaluation considered chromatographic separation methods and supplies sample/source counts, SEM where available, and confidence codes. These are compiled weighted means; underlying paper and sampling variation remains. No combined carotenoid value was split. Candidate ingestion remains behind the Stage B integrity gate.
