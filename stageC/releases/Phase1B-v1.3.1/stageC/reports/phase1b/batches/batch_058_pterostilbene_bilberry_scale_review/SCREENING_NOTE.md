# Batch 058 — pterostilbene in Slovak bilberries: unit-scale discrepancy

## Source

Habánová M, Habán M, Kobidová R, Schwarzová M, Gažo J. “Analysis of Biologically Active Substances in Bilberry (*Vaccinium myrtillus* L.) in Selected Natural Localities of Slovak Republic.” *Journal of Central European Agriculture*. 2013;14(3):1210–1219. DOI: 10.5513/JCEA01/14.3.1328. Full text: https://jcea.agr.hr/articles/774479_ANALYSIS_OF_BIOLOGICALLY_ACTIVE_SUBSTANCES_IN_BILBERRY_%28VACCINIUM_MYRTILLUS_L_%29_IN_SELECTED_NATURAL_LOCALITIES_OF_SLOVAK_REPUBL_en.pdf

## Screen finding

The paper reports pterostilbene for five wild Slovak bilberry localities and one Uruguayan supermarket blueberry sample, each mean ± SD, n=4. Table 2 and the abstract label the unit as mg/g dry weight and give 1.274–2.162 mg/g. The source describes HPLC-UV at 320 nm after methanol extraction but gives no pterostilbene-specific authentic-standard or calibration details in the accessible methods.

## Hold reason

This scale conflicts sharply with other primary Vaccinium reports located in this project’s screening history: Rimando et al. (2004) reports 99–520 ng/g dry sample, and Rimando & Barney (2005) reports 0.12–2.74 µg/g lyophilized berry. Those scales are approximately three orders of magnitude below this paper’s values. The paper also reports total polyphenols in the tens of mg/g dry weight, making the pterostilbene result unusually large as a share of the total. The unit may be mislabelled or the calibration may be unsuitable, but the accessible evidence cannot resolve which. No quantitative candidate row is prepared and no coverage is increased until the authors/source can clarify the unit, calibration and analyte identity.

## Next action

Seek an author correction, table source file or independent exact-standard pterostilbene measurement in bilberries. Treat the reported values as unresolved and do not silently rescale mg/g to µg/g.
