# Batch 053 — Scutellaria commercial tinctures (scope screen)

Gao et al. (2008) quantified baicalin, baicalein and wogonin in four commercial *Scutellaria baicalensis* root tinctures (SB1–SB4), using authentic standards and validated HPLC-UV. Table 1 reports triplicate mean ± SD in µg/mL. Converted to mg/mL (÷1000):

| Product | Baicalin | Baicalein | Wogonin |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| SB1 | 7.270 ± 0.111 | 5.880 ± 0.057 | 1.610 ± 0.003 |
| SB2 | 0.177 ± 0.0021 | 4.430 ± 0.003 | 0.112 ± 0.0029 |
| SB3 | 10.600 ± 0.190 | 0.667 ± 0.0028 | 0.262 ± 0.0040 |
| SB4 | 0.117 ± 0.0001 | 0.517 ± 0.0010 | 0.080 ± 0.0007 |

These are commercial medicinal herbal tinctures, with alcohol extraction and manufacturer-specific drug-to-solvent ratios. The paper describes *S. baicalensis* tincture as a traditional medicinal product; it does not establish ordinary food or food-ingredient status for these measured products. Phase 1B screening rule 6 excludes supplements and medicinal botanicals from verified food mappings. Therefore no food-composition candidate rows were prepared and these values do not increase accepted coverage. They are recorded as source evidence for P4/scope review only; do not transfer tincture concentration to root or decoction.

Source: Gao et al. (2008), *Journal of Pharmacy & Pharmaceutical Sciences* 11(1):77–87, DOI 10.18433/j39g6v, PMID 18445366. [Full text](https://journals.library.ualberta.ca/jpps/index.php/JPPS/article/download/1122/1150/5448).
