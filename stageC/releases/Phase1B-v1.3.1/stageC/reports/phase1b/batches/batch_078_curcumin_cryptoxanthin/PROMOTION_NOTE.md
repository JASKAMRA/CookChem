# Batch 078 — curcumin and beta-cryptoxanthin promotion

Promoted 12 rows from pre-existing screened candidates after exact ID/CID checks against the reconciled Stage B registry. Ten rows are Curcumin I in commercial turmeric powders from Paudel et al. (2025), reported as mg/g and normalized ×1000 to mg/kg. Two are USDA/NCC compiled beta-cryptoxanthin values: raw orange (3 source values, confidence B) and papaya (1 source, confidence C), reported per 100 g edible portion. Technical triplicate SD, as-analyzed powder basis, and database confidence/source-count limits are retained.

The official Stage C pipeline ingested 144 records, rebuilt 144 exact mappings, and passed Phase 1 validation. This batch adds two covered compounds.
