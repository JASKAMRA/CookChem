# Batch 027 — curcumin in commercial turmeric powder

Paudel et al. (2025), *Journal of Food Quality*, DOI 10.1155/jfq/8925770. Full text Table 2 reports Curcumin I separately for 10 commercial turmeric powders at 15.11–22.40 mg/g, with technical triplicate mean ± SD. Curcumin reference standard purity was 99.9%; the HPLC method separated Cur I, II, and III and used calibration. Individual values, sample origin, and reported manufacture-to-analysis age are transcribed into the candidate CSV. Converted mg/g to mg/kg by ×1000.

The source calls these commercial turmeric powder samples but does not specify explicit moisture correction for Table 2. Rows therefore say as-analyzed powder basis; no inferred dry-matter conversion is made. Values remain candidates pending Stage B checksum reconciliation and exact mapping.

Full text: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1155/jfq/8925770 .


## Ingestion disposition (2026-11-02)
All 10 Curcumin I records were exact-mapped to the Stage B curcumin identity and ingested. Reported mg/g was normalized to mg/kg by ×1000; source commercial-powder basis and technical-triplicate limitation are retained.
