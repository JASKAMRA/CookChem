# Batch 110 — BHT in retail edible palm oils

## Evidence decision

Three measurements are recorded as a **candidate addition** for butylated hydroxytoluene (BHT), from palm-oil samples collected in retail markets. The primary study reports 86.1 ± 0.2, 18.0 ± 0.1, and 6.8 ± 0.1 µg/g in Table 1 (Thai Journal of Toxicology 24(2), 113–118, 2009, p.117). The paper describes GC–MS analysis, a BHT reference standard, and three determinations. The table and abstract use µg/g; a conflicting mg/g statement in the body text is treated as a unit typo and is not used.

The analyte maps to V20 compound CID 31404 with the exact InChIKey `NLZUEZXRPGMBCV-UHFFFAOYSA-N`. Values are retained in the source unit and normalized to mg/kg (numerically equivalent for mass-per-mass units). The three determinations are not treated as three distinct foods or as a market-wide distribution.
