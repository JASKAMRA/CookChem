# Batch 031 — USDA raw green soybean isoflavones

## Screening decision

Prepared two database-derived candidate records for daidzein and genistein in raw green soybeans (edamame), NDB 11450. The USDA Release 2.0 table reports means of 20.34 and 22.57 mg/100 g edible portion, respectively, each based on 35 observations (SE 12.80 and 16.92; confidence code B). These are database summary values, not sample-level primary-paper results. No accepted coverage is claimed.

## Source and extraction

USDA, *Database for the Isoflavone Content of Selected Foods*, Release 2.0, official report: https://www.ars.usda.gov/ARSUserFiles/80400525/Data/isoflav/Isoflav_R2.pdf. The table gives edible-portion units and separately lists daidzein and genistein. Reported mg/100 g values are converted to mg/kg edible portion by multiplying by 10. Source reference numbers are preserved per row but not individually reconciled in this screening batch.

## Review limits

Release 2.0 has been superseded by Release 2.1. Before acceptance, compare NDB 11450 entries and definitions against R2.1 and verify exact Stage B compound-ID mappings. The compiled row lacks cultivar, geography and assay detail in its summary table; do not describe it as a single-study measurement.
