# Batch 039 — Astaxanthin and canthaxanthin in farmed Atlantic salmon

## Screening result

Two provisional candidate rows were prepared from Refsgaard et al. (1998): astaxanthin 5.5 ± 1.1 mg/kg muscle and canthaxanthin 0.200 ± 0.047 mg/kg muscle, reported for a homogeneous cohort of 145 farmed Atlantic salmon (*Salmo salar*). The values are retained exactly as abstract means and biological standard deviations. They are not ingested and do not change accepted coverage.

## Source

Refsgaard HHF, Brockhoff PB, Jensen B. “Biological Variation of Lipid Constituents and Distribution of Tocopherols and Astaxanthin in Farmed Atlantic Salmon (*Salmo salar*).” *Journal of Agricultural and Food Chemistry*. 1998;46(3):808–812. DOI: 10.1021/jf970721q. Publisher page: https://doi.org/10.1021/jf970721q.

## Review required

The accessible publisher abstract provides exact sample count and concentration statistics but not the analytical method, fillet preparation, moisture basis, salmon feed details, or carotenoid stereoisomer resolution. Stage B astaxanthin identity is stereochemically specified. Retrieve/review full text and confirm assay analyte scope and compatible edible-muscle basis before acceptance. Cohort biological SD is not a general market variability estimate. Do not interpret salmon astaxanthin as algae/supplement exposure.
