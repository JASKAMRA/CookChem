# Batch 015 — Portuguese common-bean phenolic composition

Mecha et al. (2020), DOI 10.1017/S0007114519002836, PMID 31699168. Primary full text, Table 2 and methods: https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/british-journal-of-nutrition/article/human-bioavailability-of-phenolic-compounds-found-in-common-beans-the-use-of-highresolution-ms-to-evaluate-interindividual-variability/6B5783ED557A1EF751B3D7530A22905B .

The study compared raw and cooked Moleiro (Portuguese traditional) common beans. Raw measurements used UPLC-Q-TOF-MS, authentic standards, external calibration and triplicate extractions. Values in Table 2 are µg/g raw seed dry weight. Eight direct analytes with exact Stage B identity were ingested for raw beans only: gallic, protocatechuic, 4-hydroxybenzoic, caffeic, p-coumaric, sinapic/sinapinic, quercetin and kaempferol. Unit conversion µg/g to mg/kg is 1:1.

Cooked-bean paired results are reported relative to raw seed dry weight and are intentionally excluded from Phase 1B to avoid adding cooking-retention interpretation. Generic catechin/epicatechin results are held for stereochemical identity; trans-ferulic remains pending identity compatibility. The source’s 7-person human intervention results are bioavailability data and were not used as food composition measurements. No exposure calculated.
