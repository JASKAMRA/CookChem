# Batch 019 — oleuropein in Kolovi olive fruit

## Screening decision

A compound-specific primary quantitative measurement is prepared as seven candidate records for Stage B Oleuropein (`CHEM-PUBCHEM-5281544`). Martakos et al. report oleuropein separately from oleuropein aglycone, with concentrations of 811, 855, 324, 7.0, 3.5, 10 and 5.4 mg/kg across seven Kolovi olive fruit samples.

## Source and sample scope

Martakos, I. et al. (2021). “Development of Analytical Strategies for the Determination of Olive Fruit Bioactive Compounds Using UPLC-HRMS and HPLC-DAD. Chemical Characterization of Kolovi Lesvos Variety as a Case Study.” *Molecules*, 26(23), 7182. DOI: 10.3390/molecules26237182; PMID: 34885766. Full text: https://www.mdpi.com/1420-3049/26/23/7182. Table 1 gives target screening results in mg/kg. Seven samples were hand-picked from two olive groves on Lesvos Island, Greece, in late October to mid-November 2018. Samples were pitted, freeze-dried and homogenized before analysis. The method used targeted UPLC-HRMS with standards and optimized sample preparation.

## Basis and uncertainty

The values are on the analyzed freeze-dried sample basis, because the fruit was freeze-dried before homogenization and extraction. No moisture content or fresh-weight conversion is provided, so the candidate rows retain the freeze-dried basis. The 3.5–855 mg/kg range is wide; the authors link variation to differences in fruit maturity. This candidate is not a universal olive concentration.

## Pipeline gate

Candidate CSV only. It is not appended to cumulative food composition or counted as accepted coverage until the existing Stage B package checksum mismatch is reconciled through the documented release process.


## Current disposition (2026-11-02)
All seven exact-source oleuropein rows were promoted into active Stage C coverage (Batch 077). Freeze-dried study basis is preserved; no fresh-weight conversion is inferred.
