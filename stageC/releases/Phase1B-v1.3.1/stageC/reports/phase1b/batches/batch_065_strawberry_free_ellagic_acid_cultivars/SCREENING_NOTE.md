# Batch 065 — strawberry free ellagic acid cultivar survey

Primary source: Atkinson et al. (2006), *Annals of Botany*, DOI 10.1093/aob/mcj046, PMID 16423867; [full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2803644/). Tables 1 and 2 report free ellagic acid (EA) separately from ellagitannins and hydrolysis-released total EA. This batch records all 48 positive free-EA table entries (33 from year 1, 15 from year 2); `ND` means “not determined” and is omitted.

Fruit was harvested ripe from field-grown UK East Malling breeding-program cultivars. Several fruits from each of six plants were pooled per cultivar; frozen in liquid nitrogen and stored at −80 °C. Free EA was extracted without acid hydrolysis, separated by HPLC-PDA at 254 nm and quantified against ellagic acid dehydrate. Source units are µg/g frozen weight; the normalized numeric value is identical in mg/kg frozen weight.

All rows remain held candidates: the cultivar tables provide one pooled value per cultivar without SD or assay replicate count. The frozen-weight basis is retained and must not be silently equated with fresh weight. Do not treat this breeding collection as a representative retail-food mean. Stage B checksum gate remains unresolved, so no candidates are ingested.
