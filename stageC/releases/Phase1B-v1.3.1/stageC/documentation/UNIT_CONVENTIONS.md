# Unit conventions

Mass-basis food concentrations normalize to `mg/kg`; volume-basis food concentrations normalize to `mg/L`. Amount output is `mg`. Supported mass units include g, mg, µg, ng, and kg. Supported mass-basis concentrations include mg/kg, µg/kg, mg/g, µg/g, mg/100 g, µg/100 g, and ng/g. Supported volume-basis concentrations include mg/L, µg/L, mg/mL, µg/mL, ng/mL, mg/100 mL, and µg/100 mL. Conversion uses exact decimal factors and preserves source value/unit in the record and provenance. No density is inferred between mass and volume bases. Exposure calculations require a compatible mass-basis concentration and food mass; volume-basis records remain usable for coverage but cannot produce mass-based exposure estimates without additional compatible inputs.

Volume units are rejected because density and matrix-specific support are absent. Molar conversion is available only with verified identity and molecular weight. Wet- and dry-weight records are never interconverted; concentration and ingredient mass must have a compatible explicitly recorded food basis. Negative and non-finite values are rejected. Unit conversion factors and implementation are in `src/stage_c/unit_conversion.py`.
