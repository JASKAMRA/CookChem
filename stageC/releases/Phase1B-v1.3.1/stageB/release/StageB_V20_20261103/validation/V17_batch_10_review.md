# V17 Batch 10 review

Accepted: S-equol (PubChem CID 91469, (3S), InChIKey ADFCQWZHKCXPAJ-GFCCVEGCSA-N), registered as MOL-V17-CID-91469. Primary PMID 29934673 tests S-equol as an OATP2B1 inhibitor; Ki 18.6 ± 5.6 μM and substrate-specific E3S IC50 results were verified from the authors’ dissertation, Figures 21–22. The journal article full text was not independently accessed, so this limitation is retained. A separate primary human study (PMID 15883431) establishes S-equol as a soy-isoflavone-derived intestinal bacterial metabolite. This is conditional metabolite context, not direct soy-food content or universal production.

No clinical drug interaction is inferred. Warning V17-W03 remains open. Batch gate pending.
