# V17 Batch 9 review

Accepted: Sunset Yellow FCF (PubChem CID 17730; InChIKey OIQPTROHQCGFEF-UHFFFAOYSA-L), registered as MOL-V17-CID-17730. The primary paper and supplemental Table S2.1 report an OATP2B1 inhibitor Ki of 68.4 μM (95% CI 58.3–80.4 μM). The finding is in vitro. FDA’s color additive listing establishes a food-use regulatory context, but not occurrence in a specific food or consumed dose.

Held from this lead family: FD&C Red No. 3 was not admitted because the PubChem CID reviewed represents the neutral free-acid structure while food-grade FD&C Red No. 3 is described as a sodium salt/color additive preparation; the exact study test material-to-regulatory preparation mapping was not resolved. D&C-only dye hits were excluded as non-food colorants.

No clinical Sunset Yellow interaction or food-exposure threshold is inferred. Batch gate: PASS; structural/provenance validation and 8 focused tests passed. Bibliography row and study ID were reused, and the study’s linked Evidence_ID list was extended without duplicating the publication.
