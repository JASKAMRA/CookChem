# Batch 025 — separate lutein and zeaxanthin in raw spinach

Chung, Rasmussen & Johnson (2004), *Journal of Nutrition* 134:1887–1893, DOI 10.1093/jn/134.8.1887, PMID 15284371. The study reports spinach contained 6.89 ± 0.08 mg lutein and 0.22 ± 0.01 mg zeaxanthin per 100 g wet weight (n=7), measured in the spinach used in a controlled feeding study. Prepared two separate candidate records, converted ×10 to mg/kg fresh weight. These are analyte-specific food measurements; no combined lutein+zeaxanthin value was split or inferred. The candidates are not ingested and do not change accepted coverage while the Stage B checksum gate remains unresolved.
