# Batch 064 — Naringin in grapefruit juice

## Candidate values
VanderMolen et al. (2013) quantified naringin in five grapefruit juice products using UPLC-PDA and an authentic standard. Four ordinary product samples are recorded here: Juice A 412 ± 15 µmol/L; Juice C frozen concentrate with added calcium 1182 ± 16 µmol/L; Juice D (Florida’s Natural Ruby Red) 371 ± 15 µmol/L; Juice E (Simply Grapefruit) 381 ± 15 µmol/L. Source reports concentrations adjusted for the 5-fold concentrated extract to the original juice. Using naringin MW 580.53 g/mol, these convert to 239.178 ± 8.708, 686.186 ± 9.288, 215.377 ± 8.708, and 221.182 ± 8.708 mg/L, respectively. Values remain tied to the specific products; Juice C is a concentrate.

Juice B is omitted because it was made from Juice A by solvent/resin/column processing to remove furanocoumarins; it is not a usual market product.

## Method and review flags
The source purchased naringin standard at ≥96.8% purity. UPLC-PDA on HSS C18 detected flavonoids at 310 nm; peak UV spectra and co-injection confirmed analytes. Calibration R² was 0.999, and reported sample triplicates are analytical only. The authors report the UPLC flavonoid values were approximately twice earlier HPLC values, with the reason unexplored; therefore all four candidates are held for analytical method-comparability review. Stage B checksum gate also blocks ingestion.
