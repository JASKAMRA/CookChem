# Batch 076 — positional dicaffeoylquinic acids in experimental artichoke heads

## Source and measurements

Tarantino et al. (2020), *Agronomy* 10:53, DOI 10.3390/agronomy10010053, analyzed edible globe-artichoke heads of cultivar Violetto di Provenza across three head-yield classes and three irrigation treatments in two growing seasons. Table 3 reports treatment marginal means (±SE) on a dry-matter basis. Table 3 values are retained as nine rows per target (three treatments × three head classes); no raw observations are invented.

| Target | Reported range across treatment/head-class means (mg/100 g DW) |
|---|---:|
| 1,3-diCQA | 0.7–2.0 |
| 1,5-diCQA | 176.1–412.8 |

The source names commercial standards for both positional isomers and explicitly identifies 1,3-di-O-caffeoylquinic acid (cynarin) and 1,5-di-O-caffeoylquinic acid. However, amounts for mono- and dicaffeoylquinic acids were calculated using 5-caffeoylquinic acid as reference. Rows therefore remain candidate-only pending review of surrogate calibration comparability. The study used three replicate plots per irrigation treatment and analyzed edible heads, but Table 3 values are marginal means; they are not raw sample-level records. Experimental wastewater-irrigation conditions are preserved.

## Decision

18 exact-target candidates prepared (nine for each positional target), normalized from mg/100 g DW to mg/kg DW by ×10. No conversion to fresh weight and no Stage B or accepted-data edits. Accepted coverage is unchanged.

Source: [Tarantino et al. 2020 full text, Methods 2.1–2.3.2 and Table 3](https://www.mdpi.com/2073-4395/10/1/53).
