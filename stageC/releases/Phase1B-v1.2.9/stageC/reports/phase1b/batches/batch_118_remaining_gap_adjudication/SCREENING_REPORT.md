# Batch 118 — final targeted adjudication of the eight remaining gaps

## Accepted: Eupatorin

Ivanescu et al. quantified eupatorin in authenticated *Artemisia vulgaris* aerial parts at **0.97 µg/g dry weight** (Table 2). The paper used LC-MS/MS with an authentic eupatorin standard and reports results per dry plant material. A. vulgaris is independently documented as a culinary herb with edible leaves (Kew Plants of the World Online; Singapore NParks). We accept this as a food-ingredient botanical measurement, while preserving that the analytical matrix was composite aerial parts harvested for a medicinal-plant study, not a prepared dish or commercial food. No leaf-only, fresh-weight, or serving concentration is inferred. Primary source: Ivanescu et al., *European Chemical Bulletin* 2016;5(4):119–123, DOI [10.17628/ECB.2016.5.119](https://doi.org/10.17628/ECB.2016.5.119). Method/source: [full text](https://www.bibliomed.org/mnsfulltext/246/246-1645015863.pdf). Food-use corroboration: [Kew POWO](https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/urn:lsid:ipni.org:names:20812-2/general-information), [NParks](https://www.nparks.gov.sg/florafaunaweb/flora/1/6/1689).

## Seven remaining gaps retained

- **Oroxylin A:** fresh edible Thai *Oroxylum indicum* pods were screened and the compound was isolated, but the paper reports isolation yield (19 mg from 100 g pods), not a validated native concentration. A separate newer HPLC study reports percent of extracts from immature/green fruit and pod coats; those are extract-basis results and do not establish edible portion concentration. [Thai edible-plant primary study](https://journal.waocp.org/article_89560_f1b7b2ebd250adb4a4d54c7946a57672.pdf), [fruit-extract primary study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10574379/).
- **7-Methoxyflavanone:** fresh search did not verify an exact-structure, calibrated food concentration. Broad orange-juice flavanone totals or similarly named compounds are not substitutes.
- **Amentoflavone:** a validated HPLC-PDA study reports quantitative measurement in edible Garcinia fruits, but its exact sample-level amentoflavone results table was not accessible. No numbers were inferred from abstracts. [Primary article](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfca.2011.10.006).
- **5,4′-Dihydroxy-3,6,7,8,3′-pentamethoxyflavone:** source leads establish citrus occurrence, but no exact-analyte quantitative ordinary-food value was verified.
- **Dodecyl gallate and octyl gallate:** surveys/method papers located give non-detections, analytical performance, or spiked/recovery data; those do not supply a positive native-food concentration for either compound. [Bakery method abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9809448/), [EFSA opinion on octyl gallate](https://doi.org/10.2903/j.efsa.2015.4248).
- **Prunin:** orange by-product papers report values using surrogate calibration curves (vanillic/chlorogenic/ferulic acids, quercetin, rutin), without prunin-specific standard calibration; dormant peach buds and enzyme-generated prunin are not ordinary food measurements. Kept pending. [Orange by-product study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8158112/).
