# Batch 034 — Naringin and neohesperidin in fresh clementine juice

## Screening decision

Prepared six exact-analyte candidates: naringin and neohesperidin, each in three fresh-squeezed clementine juices from Cetraro (JD), Rosarno (JE), and Corigliano Calabro (JF), Calabria, Italy. Table 6 reports means ± SD (n=3 analytical replicates), mg/100 mL juice. Naringin: 1.73 ± 0.18, 2.12 ± 1.19, and 3.14 ± 1.32. Neohesperidin: 110.63 ± 5.52, 80.26 ± 4.83, and 112.32 ± 5.63. Values were normalized to mg/L by ×10. These are site juice samples with technical assay replicates, not independent lots; the source collected 25 fruits per region.

## Source and method

Leporini M, Loizzo MR, Sicari V, Pellicanò TM, Reitano A, Dugay A, Deguin B, Tundis R. “Citrus × Clementina Hort. Juice Enriched with Its By-Products (Peels and Leaves): Chemical Composition, In Vitro Bioactivity, and Impact of Processing.” *Antioxidants*. 2020;9(4):298. DOI: 10.3390/antiox9040298; PMID: 32260119. Full text: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7222210/. Fruit was washed, peeled and squeezed; juice was centrifuged and filtered. HPLC-DAD used a C18 column and 287 nm monitoring; both target analytes were among the selected standards and retention-time/spectral matching was used.

## Scope and limitations

The candidates refer to the standard fresh-squeezed juices before any by-product enrichment or pasteurization. The SD is from triplicate analytical measurements and is not biological-lot variability. Preserve the reported volume basis; do not convert to mg/kg without juice-density evidence. Candidates remain pending Stage B exact-ID mapping and checksum-gated ingestion.
