# Batch 120 — Amentoflavone in melon seed oil

## Accepted record

Mallek-Ayadi et al. report **3.88 ± 0.11 mg/100 g** amentoflavone in oil from *Cucumis melo* L. Maazoun cultivar seeds (Table 4; triplicate determinations). The equivalent mean is 38.8 mg/kg oil, with SD 1.1 mg/kg. The primary article describes extraction of the oil from Tunisian-grown Maazoun seeds and HPLC analysis of the phenolic fraction. It uses 34 commercial phenolic standards, compares peak retention times with corresponding standards, and reports the phenolic content on an oil/dry-weight sample basis.

## Eligibility decision

Accepted as **food ingredient**. The article describes the oil as edible, tests its sensory properties, and states it can be used for culinary preparations and food formulations. This expands the prior supplement/medicinal-only context for the compound with direct food-ingredient evidence. The value is specific to one experimental cultivar sample; triplicate analytical determinations do not establish biological or market variability. No extrapolation to other melon cultivars, raw melon, or consumer exposure is made.

Source: Mallek-Ayadi S, Bahloul N, Kechaou N. 2019. *Grasas y Aceites* 70(1), e284. DOI: https://doi.org/10.3989/gya.0215181. See Table 4 and Methods 2.1, 2.3, 2.9, 2.11; the authors' culinary-use conclusion appears in section 3.11.
