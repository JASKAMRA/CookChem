# Batch 051 — Hyperoside in pure apple juice

Li et al. (2023) report five measured hyperoside values among seven 100% pure apple juice products (PAJ1–PAJ5): 0.0447, 0.0120, 0.0544, 0.1399 and 0.0315 mg/L. PAJ6 and PAJ7 were ND and were not converted to zero or added as quantitative candidate records.

The study used an exact hyperoside reference standard, UPLC-MS/MS in negative ESI/MRM mode, with calibration R²=0.99568. Juice preparation: 400 μL juice plus 600 μL acetonitrile, centrifugation, supernatant drying and reconstitution. Products were sampled from Chinese retail stores/supermarkets over January–March 2021; each representative product sample was based on at least three samples, but product-specific replicate dispersion and pooling details are not fully reported. Values stay on the source's mg/L juice basis.

Source: Li et al. (2023), *Food Science & Nutrition* 11:4093–4099, DOI 10.1002/fsn3.3399, PMID 37457181. [Full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10345717/). Candidates await mapping review and gated ingestion.
