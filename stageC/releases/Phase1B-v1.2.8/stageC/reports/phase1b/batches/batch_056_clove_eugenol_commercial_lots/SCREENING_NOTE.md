# Batch 056 — eugenol in commercial clove spice lots

## Source

Sgorbini B, Bicchi C, Cagliero C, Cordero C, Liberto E, Rubiolo P. “Herbs and spices: characterization and quantitation of biologically-active markers for routine quality control by multiple headspace solid-phase microextraction combined with separative or non-separative analysis.” *Journal of Chromatography A*. 2015;1376:9–17. DOI: 10.1016/j.chroma.2014.12.007. Author manuscript: https://iris.unito.it/handle/2318/152493

## Screen outcome

The study analyzed ten commercial clove lots supplied by Cannamela (Italy). Table 3 gives separative MHS-SPME-GC-MS eugenol values in mg/g. The article identifies eugenol using an authentic standard and quantifies marker concentration directly from solid clove material. Ten sample-level candidate records are retained and normalized to mg/kg (×1,000). No range endpoints were split or extrapolated.

Values, lots 1–10: 149, 142, 313, 347, 240, 150, 261, 283, 162, 108 mg/g.

## Hold / review

These are commercial clove spice lots, but the article does not explicitly state whether they were dried/as-sold or report moisture content. Values therefore remain candidates on an as-supplied solid-matrix basis and do not increase accepted coverage. The ten lots are not treated as biological replicates from one cultivar. Stage B checksum gate still blocks connector ingestion.


## Ingestion disposition (2026-11-02)
Eligible mass-based quantitative records from this batch were exact-mapped and promoted in Batch 080. Original measurement basis and sampling limitations remain as described above. Volume-only measurements, if any, were not converted without density.
