# Accepted, held, and rejected screening report

Accepted: six USDA Release 3.3 aggregate measurement rows across four compounds. The original four accepted rows are in batches 001–002; batch 003 adds EGCG and theaflavin for USDA NDB 99069 standardized green tea infusion. Each has six corresponding active mapping rows in total, with exact or manually reviewed Stage B identity.

Held: seven candidate records remain in `CANDIDATES.csv`: apple quercetin; fresh-parsley apigenin, kaempferol, myricetin, luteolin; raw-orange-juice naringenin and hesperetin. The USDA flavonoid values are aggregate means, with glycoside-to-aglycone conversion creating an analyte-basis ambiguity that the current measurement schema cannot represent. The myricetin candidate also has N=4 and confidence C.

Rejected from ingestion: parsley isorhamnetin is recorded as a USDA measured mean of 0.00 mg/100g (N=4, confidence C). It is a reported zero/below-detection result, not missing data; the project requires a positive quantitative mean for accepted exposure measurements.

The tea aggregates have N=2, confidence B, and refer to an unpublished 2002 industry data summary (USDA ref 272). Limits remain explicit. No cooking, recipe, bioavailability, or risk interpretation is included.
