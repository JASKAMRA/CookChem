# Food-composition coverage report

Active dataset: 6 verified quantitative records, 4 unique Stage B compounds, 3 food-use classes, and 6 mappings. Quantitative compound coverage is 4/119 (3.36%); 115 Stage B compounds have no accepted quantitative concentration in this dataset. Coverage is deliberately narrow and does not establish absence in other foods.

Batch 001: raw apple with skin and batch 002: unsweetened dry cocoa powder, each with USDA catechin and epicatechin means as documented in their batch reports. Batch 003: USDA NDB 99069, tea green brewed decaffeinated, EGCG 26.05 and theaflavin 0.12 mg/100g (100ml) as-consumed standardized 1% infusion; each N=2, SE 0.69 / 0.08, respectively, USDA confidence B, source ref 272 unpublished data. Tea estimates apply only to the USDA standardized beverage basis.

No glycoside/aglycone-equivalent flavonoid data were ingested as free aglycone values. No cooking-retention, recipe dilution, or bioavailability adjustments are applied.
