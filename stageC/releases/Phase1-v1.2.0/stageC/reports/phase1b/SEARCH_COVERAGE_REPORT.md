# Search coverage report

The Phase 1B register contains 119 Stage B compounds. Search log: 15 documented compound/source screening rows. Batches 001–003 include six accepted USDA aggregate measurements covering four compounds: (+)-catechin, (-)-epicatechin, (-)-epigallocatechin 3-gallate (EGCG), and theaflavin.

All ten P2 compounds received a source screen. Four P2 flavonoid entries remain held for aglycone-equivalent ambiguity (apigenin, kaempferol, myricetin, luteolin), two citrus flavanones remain held for aglycone-equivalent semantics (naringenin, hesperetin), and parsley isorhamnetin is a measured aggregate zero (N=4, CC C), recorded but not ingested under the positive-value acceptance rule. Quercetin remains held because its aggregate combines free and converted analyte bases. EGCG and theaflavin were accepted on the standardized infusion basis.

P3 caffeine screening identified official USDA SR Legacy NDB 14209 brewed coffee and the official April 2018 downloadable dataset. The value was not ingested because the official component row was not extracted; a mirrored old SR26 report is insufficient on its own. Continue with the official SR Legacy data file.
