# Batch 035 — Glycyrrhizinic acid in Danish licorice foods

## Screening decision

Prepared four category-summary candidate records from a Danish governmental food surveillance study: licorice confectionery A (excluding pure extract products; n=137, mean 1043 mg/kg, range <LOQ–4936), pure licorice-extract confectionery B (n=8, mean 17,922 mg/kg, range 14,935–23,154), licorice ice cream (n=15, mean 920 mg/kg, range 408–1690), and brewed licorice tea (n=59, mean 133 mg/L, range <LOQ–1203). The study measured glycyrrhizinic acid (GA) by UPLC-UV. Values are aggregate category means, not individual product records. No GA-to-glycyrrhizin or salt conversion was applied.

## Source

Ballin NZ et al. “Glycyrrhizinic acid in licorice products on the Danish market.” *Food Control*. 2023;143:109322. DOI: 10.1016/j.foodcont.2022.109322. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.foodcont.2022.109322. The study sampled 219 confectionery, ice cream and tea products in Denmark from 2019–2022 and used an accredited UPLC-UV method. The paper reports category-level means, ranges and sample counts.

## Identity and data limits

The target list names glycyrrhizin (CID 14982); the paper reports glycyrrhizinic acid (GA). The chemically equivalent acid/salt/form mapping must be checked against Stage B before acceptance. Category summaries include censored <LOQ results in some categories; confirm source treatment before ingestion. Tea is a volume-basis beverage and remains mg/L. Candidate records remain outside active data pending mapping review and the Stage B checksum gate.


## Ingestion disposition (2026-11-02)
Eligible mass-based quantitative records from this batch were exact-mapped and promoted in Batch 080. Original measurement basis and sampling limitations remain as described above. Volume-only measurements, if any, were not converted without density.
