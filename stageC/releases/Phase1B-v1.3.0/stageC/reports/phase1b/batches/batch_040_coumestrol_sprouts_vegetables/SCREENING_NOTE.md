# Batch 040 — Coumestrol in sprouts and vegetables

## Screening decision

Prepared 14 source-specific candidate rows for the exact Stage B Coumestrol target (MOL-V10-5281707): six supermarket vegetable matrices from Fig. 3 and eight commercial soybean-sprout records (four products × uncultivated/controlled cultivation) from Table 3 of Ohta et al. (2021). Reported means ± SD are n=3. Concentrations remain on the paper's dry-material basis; µg/g is numerically equivalent to mg/kg. No fresh-weight conversion or population-level extrapolation was made.

## Source and method

Ohta T, Uto T, Tanaka H. “Effective methods for increasing coumestrol in soybean sprouts.” *PLOS ONE*. 2021;16(11):e0260147. DOI: 10.1371/journal.pone.0260147; PMID: 34793574. Full text: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8601530/.

Vegetables were purchased from supermarkets in Japan, lyophilized, powdered and analyzed. Samples were extracted in methanol at 50°C for 1 h and measured by HPLC-UV at 343 nm using an authentic coumestrol standard (≥95%); analyte identity was checked by standard retention time. Calibration R²=0.9999 and mean recovery 97.91% (RSD 1.87%). Table 3 product sprouts were either lyophilized without cultivation or cultivated in tap water under light at 24°C for one day before lyophilization.

## Limitations and review

These are dry-weight candidate measurements, not fresh edible-weight concentrations. Product identities are anonymized as A–D, and the controlled-cultivation values represent an experimental treatment rather than ordinary market composition. The paper reports n=3; it does not establish broad commercial, seasonal or geographic variability. Exact target name/CID appear to match, but records still need Stage B mapping review and the existing checksum gate before ingestion.


## Ingestion disposition (2026-11-02)
Eligible mass-based quantitative records from this batch were exact-mapped and promoted in Batch 080. Original measurement basis and sampling limitations remain as described above. Volume-only measurements, if any, were not converted without density.
