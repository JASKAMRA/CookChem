# Batch 071 — Pterostilbene in Vaccinium cultivar samples

## Primary-source verification

Rimando and Cody’s author article, “Determination of Stilbenes in Blueberries,” *LCGC North America* 23(11):1192–1200 (1 Nov 2005), is available at [LCGC](https://www.chromatographyonline.com/view/determination-stilbenes-blueberries-0). The article describes whole-berry samples, including lyophilized U.S. samples, GC–MS in selected-ion-monitoring mode, and quantitation against a synthetic pterostilbene standard. It reports a pterostilbene LOD of 18 ng/g and LOQ of 61 ng/g dry sample, and states that pterostilbene occurred in three samples: two *Vaccinium ashei* rabbiteye cultivars and one *V. stamineum* deerberry sample, in the 99–520 ng/g DW range.

A 2018 review, “The stilbene profile in edible berries,” Table 1, transcribes the three cultivar-level values from Rimando and Cody (2005): Tifblue, 0.151 µg/g DW; Climax, 0.099 µg/g DW; deerberry selection SHF3A-2-108, 0.520 µg/g DW. All exceed the LCGC article’s reported LOQ, and their species/overall range agree with the author article. The review is the accessible source for the exact cultivar-to-value assignments in this pass; the original LCGC Table III is presented as a non-text table and its numeric cells have not yet been directly checked.

## Provenance correction and hold

The earlier candidate CSV incorrectly attached DOI 10.1021/jf040095e, which belongs to the separate 2004 *Journal of Agricultural and Food Chemistry* article, not the 2005 LCGC article. That DOI has been removed from Batch 071. The 2005 article is cited by journal, volume, issue, pages, and linked directly. Exact cultivar values remain held until the original Table III is checked directly and sample-level n is clarified. Values remain on dry-weight basis; no fresh-weight conversion is made.
