# Batch 048 — Grapefruit furanocoumarins

## Candidate records

Prepared five records:

- Epoxybergamottin in fresh peeled grapefruit edible flesh: 1121.7 ± 1053.3 ng/g (n=3).
- Epoxybergamottin in commercial grapefruit juice: 3.2 ± 1.0 ng/g (n=3).
- Bergamottin in fresh peeled grapefruit edible flesh: 8356.7 ± 583.6 ng/g (n=3).
- Bergamottin in commercial grapefruit juice: 4289.2 ± 843.5 ng/g (n=3).
- Paradisin C in 12 grapefruit juice preparations used in a clinical study: 0.59 ± 0.17 µg/mL (n=12; %CV 28.3).

For Melough et al., ng/g values were normalized to mg/kg (×0.001); juice mass concentration remains mass-based and was not converted to volume. For Hanley et al., µg/mL is retained and shown as mg/L without density conversion.

## Primary sources and methods

Melough MM, Lee SG, Cho E, et al. “Identification and Quantitation of Furocoumarins in Popularly Consumed Foods in the U.S. Using QuEChERS Extraction Coupled with UPLC-MS/MS Analysis.” *J Agric Food Chem.* 2017;65(24):5049–5055. DOI: 10.1021/acs.jafc.7b01279; PMID: 28581738. Full text: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8070520/. Three samples from different retailers/brands per food were analyzed. Fresh grapefruit peel was removed to isolate edible flesh. Authentic standards were used. QuEChERS extraction and UPLC-MS/MS MRM were reported; table concentrations are means (SD) from three samples. LOD/LOQ and calibration information are reported.

Hanley MJ, Masse G, Harmatz JS, et al. “Effect of blueberry juice on clearance of buspirone and flurbiprofen in human volunteers.” *Br J Clin Pharmacol.* 2013;75(4):1041–1052. DOI: 10.1111/j.1365-2125.2012.04450.x; PMID: 22943633. Full text: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3612722/. Table 3 reports mean, SD and CV for Paradisin C in 12 grapefruit juice preparations used as a clinical-study comparator.
