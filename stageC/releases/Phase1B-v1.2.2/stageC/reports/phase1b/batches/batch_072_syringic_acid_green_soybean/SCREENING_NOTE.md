# Batch 072 — syringic acid fractions in green soy bean

## Source and measurements

Zhao et al. (2017), “Characterization of Free, Conjugated, and Bound Phenolic Acids in Seven Commonly Consumed Vegetables,” *Molecules* 22:1878 (DOI 10.3390/molecules22111878; PMID 29104269), reports green-soy-bean values from Beijing, Hangzhou and Guangzhou. Tables 2 and 3 report conjugated and bound syringic acid in mg/kg fresh weight (FW), mean ± SD, n=3. The authors list syringic acid among Sigma-Aldrich HPLC-quality standards, quantified the analytes by UPLC-MS/MS, and sampled five stalls per region; each regional 2-kg pool was divided into three biological replicates.

| Fraction | Beijing | Hangzhou | Guangzhou |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Conjugated | 102.46 ± 3.58 | 87.45 ± 3.15 | 89.84 ± 3.24 |
| Bound | 45.10 ± 2.18 | 33.40 ± 1.97 | 36.87 ± 1.24 |

All values are mg/kg FW. Conjugated values are the alkaline-hydrolyzed soluble fraction minus the prior free fraction; bound values are released by alkaline hydrolysis of the methanol-insoluble residue. The paper did not report a free-form syringic-acid value.

## Screening decision

Six exact-standard candidate rows are prepared, keeping conjugated and bound fractions separate. They are held because hydrolysis-released fractions do not represent native free syringic acid, and the Stage B checksum gate still prevents ingestion. Do not sum the fractions or count this as accepted coverage.

Source: [PMC full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6150285/); Table 2, Table 3, Sections 3.2–3.6.
