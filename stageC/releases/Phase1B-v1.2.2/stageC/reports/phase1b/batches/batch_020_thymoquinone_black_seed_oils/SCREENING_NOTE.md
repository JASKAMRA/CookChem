# Batch 020 — thymoquinone in commercial black seed oils

## Screening decision

Six compound-specific primary measurements are prepared as candidate records for Stage B Thymoquinone (`CHEM-PUBCHEM-10281`), whose Stage B context is *Nigella sativa* seed oil. Khaikin et al. report oil samples 1–6 at 133.9, 538.2, 172.4, 138.9, 809.4 and 45.26 mg/100 g (mean analytical results), respectively. Candidate normalized values are multiplied by 10 to express mg/kg.

## Source and method

Khaikin, E., Chrubasik-Hausmann, S., Kaya, S., & Zimmermann, B.F. (2022). “Screening of Thymoquinone Content in Commercial *Nigella sativa* Products to Identify a Promising and Safe Study Medication.” *Nutrients*, 14(17), 3501. DOI: 10.3390/nu14173501; PMID: 36079759. Full text: https://www.mdpi.com/2072-6643/14/17/3501. Table 1 identifies the bottled-oil products as originating from Egypt. Table 3 reports mean TQ concentration, SD, and analytical relative SD. The paper used a validated HPLC-UV method with 99.9% analytical TQ standard and methanolic extraction.

## Scope and uncertainty

These are six individual retail oil products, not a representative mean for all *Nigella sativa* oils. Values range from 45.26 to 809.4 mg/100 g. Sample 6 also lists rosemary extract and tocopherols; the other bottled products are described as oil with no further ingredients. The source says duplicate analytical results except oil sample 2, which was analyzed in decuplicate.

## Pipeline gate

Candidate CSV only. It is not appended to cumulative food composition or counted as accepted coverage until the existing Stage B package checksum mismatch is reconciled through the documented release process.


## Current disposition (2026-11-02)
Five commercial oil samples were promoted into active Stage C coverage (Batch 077). Sample 6 remains excluded because it lists rosemary extract and tocopherols; product-specific technical replicates do not represent independent lots.
