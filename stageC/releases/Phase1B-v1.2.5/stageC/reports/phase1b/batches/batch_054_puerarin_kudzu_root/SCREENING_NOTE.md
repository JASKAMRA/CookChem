# Batch 054 — Puerarin in food-industry kudzu root

Zhao et al. (2014) analyzed *Pueraria lobata* roots supplied by Suizhou Eryuefeng Food Co. and dried to constant weight at 70 °C. The paper reports that puerarin decreases from 72.07 g/kg for the youngest root age (1 year) to 20.90 g/kg at 5 years. Only those exact textual endpoints are represented as two candidates; intermediate figure values were not estimated.

The source used an authentic puerarin standard and HPLC-UV (C18, 250 nm). Dry root powder was extracted in 70% ethanol by ultrasound. All tests were performed in triplicate, but the paper does not clearly distinguish analytical repeats from independent biological samples. Values remain on dry-weight basis: 72.07 and 20.90 g/kg = 72,070 and 20,900 mg/kg.

The root was supplied by a food company and the article discusses selection of roots for starch/product processing; candidate classification is `food ingredient`, pending reviewer confirmation that this ingredient use meets Phase 1B scope. Stage B identity was checked. The records remain candidates pending mapping review and the Stage B checksum gate.

Source: Zhao et al. (2014), *Journal of Chemistry*, Article 582176, DOI 10.1155/2014/582176. [Full article](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1155/2014/582176).
