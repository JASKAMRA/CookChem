# Batch 033 — Additional targeted flavonoids in Greek honey

## Screening decision

Prepared 13 quantitative candidate rows from the same primary study as batch 032: genistein (four positive matrix medians), galangin (five medians), and pinocembrin (four positive matrix medians). All values are mg/kg honey and are group medians, not individual samples. The table lists fir-honey genistein and thyme-honey pinocembrin as below LOD; those cells were excluded rather than converted to zero or estimated. Candidates remain pending Stage B mapping and release gate.

## Source and methods

Koulis GA et al. *Molecules*. 2022;27(14):4444. DOI: 10.3390/molecules27144444. https://www.mdpi.com/1420-3049/27/14/4444. Table 1 gives targeted LC-HRMS analyte medians for Greek blossom, fir, oak, pine and thyme honeys. Honey sample preparation and study context are documented in batch 032. The article reports use of analytical standards for target quantitation.

## Caveats

Group medians and sample sizes are retained without inventing individual measurements. Below-LOD entries are omitted. Exact Stage B mapping must be checked before potential ingestion; these candidates do not increase accepted coverage.


## Ingestion disposition (2026-11-02)
Eligible mass-based quantitative records from this batch were exact-mapped and promoted in Batch 080. Original measurement basis and sampling limitations remain as described above. Volume-only measurements, if any, were not converted without density.
