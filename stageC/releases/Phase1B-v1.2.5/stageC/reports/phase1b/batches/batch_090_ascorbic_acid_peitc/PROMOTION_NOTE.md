# Batch 090 — ascorbic acid and PEITC food measurements

Promoted one broccoli ascorbic-acid value (131 mg/100 g fresh weight) from a primary HPLC study and two broccoli-floret PEITC values (5.3 ± 0.06 and 5.6 ± 0.14 µg/g dry weight; numerically mg/kg DW) from a primary GC-MS study. The source extraction/pH stability concern for ascorbic acid, PEITC sample handling, and unclear biological replication are retained in record-level uncertainty notes.

PEITC identity was checked against Stage B CID/full InChIKey and independent FDA/NIST records; Stage B was not edited. Official ingestion and mapping are included in the active Phase 1 validation.
