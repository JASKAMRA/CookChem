# Batch 050 — Hesperetin in orange juice

Franke et al. (2005) report hesperetin aglycone at 7 ± 0.7 mg/kg in not-from-concentrate orange juice, separately from hesperidin at 315 ± 25.3 mg/kg. The study states that citrus flavanones occur predominantly as glycosides, with less than 3% as aglycones. The exact aglycone target is therefore retained separately; no glycoside-equivalent conversion or pooling was done.

Table 1 summarizes juice composition; the footnote describes variation across two batches and four samples. The juice was a chilled not-from-concentrate commercial product used in a dietary intervention. The paper identifies purchased hesperetin and hesperidin standards and HPLC/LC-PDA/ESI-MS instrumentation, but the precise juice quantification workflow needs method-level review before acceptance.
