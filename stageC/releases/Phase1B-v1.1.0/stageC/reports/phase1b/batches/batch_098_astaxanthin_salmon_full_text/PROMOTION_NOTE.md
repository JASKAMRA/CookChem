# Batch 098 — Salmon astaxanthin

Promoted six 12°C Atlantic salmon muscle treatment means from Table 4 of Ytrestøyl et al. (2025). The study sampled six fish per tank; data are means per tank ± SEM (N=3 tanks). Muscle astaxanthin is measured by HPLC with a crystalline all-E-astaxanthin reference standard. Samples are muscle from a specified fillet location. The paper reports mg/kg muscle but does not state fresh/dry denominator, so values remain source-unit and are not used for fresh-weight exposure. These are controlled feeding-trial values, not representative market means.

Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11952918/ (DOI 10.1155/anu/3454274).
