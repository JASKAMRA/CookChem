# Batch 042 — Vanillin in Ugandan vanilla pod batches

## Screening decision

Prepared five source-specific candidates from Table 1 of Neves et al. (2026), using the actual HPLC reference values for external-validation batches 10, 14, 15, 23 and 26: 11.44, 6.90, 35.73, 5.56 and 12.65 mg/g original sample. The paper says each HPLC value is based on a composite of three pods per batch and triplicate injections. Values are converted to mg/kg original sample (×1000); no dry-basis correction was applied because the paper's batch-specific HPLC sample moisture values are not provided. These are measured HPLC reference values, not NIR predictions.

## Source and method

Neves MDG, Pfeifer F, Siesler HW, Bec KB, Grabska J, Rutzinger P, Huck CW, Wise BM, Palacios MA. “Scanner-Independent Calibrations of Handheld Near-Infrared Spectra for Robust Quality Assessment of Vanilla Pods.” *Journal of Food Quality*. 2026; Article 5564134. DOI: 10.1155/jfq/5564134. Full text: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1155/jfq/5564134.

Thirty-nine vanilla pod batches came from one Ugandan farm. HPLC used a Shimadzu Nexera-i LC-2040C 3D with UV detection at 190 nm and a C18 column. Three pods were combined per HPLC composite; 500 mg homogenate was extracted in 80% methanol ultrasonically for 20 h. A high-purity USP/NF/Ph. Eur.-tested vanillin standard was used, with retention-time matching. Table 1 lists the five actual HPLC values used for independent model testing.

## Limitations and inconsistencies

No cultivar, region within Uganda, specific curing state or batch-specific moisture is reported for the HPLC composites. The paper gives inconsistent broad concentration summaries: the abstract/materials section gives a maximum of 38.2 mg/g, while §2.4 says roughly 10.9–42.4 mg/g; this is flagged and not used to generate candidates. Table 1's five values are transcribed directly. Records await exact Stage B mapping and checksum-gated ingestion.


## Ingestion disposition (2026-11-02)
Eligible mass-based quantitative records from this batch were exact-mapped and promoted in Batch 080. Original measurement basis and sampling limitations remain as described above. Volume-only measurements, if any, were not converted without density.
