# Batch 017 — folic acid in ready-to-eat cereals

## Screening decision

**Twelve exact-mapped primary food-composition candidates prepared; they are eligible for mapping review and held only at the Stage B release checksum gate.** Phillips et al. measured chemically specific folic acid—not total food folate—in 12 commercial ready-to-eat cereal products using validated LC-MS with a 13C-folic acid internal standard. Table II reports each anonymized product's mean, SE, RSD and observed range in µg/100 g as-sold product. Each product had four regional composites; each composite pooled cereals from three retail locations. Product descriptions and the authors' anonymized A–L labels are retained; no commercial brand is inferred.

All 12 rows are now explicitly mapped to the complete Stage B identity for folic acid (`CHEM-PUBCHEM-135398658`, CID 135398658, InChIKey OVBPIULPVIDEAO-LBPRGKRZSA-N). The source’s analyte-specific LC-MS method and fortified cereal matrix support exact mapping. ## Source and sample scope

Phillips KM et al. (2010), “Folic Acid Content of Ready-to-Eat Cereals Determined by Liquid Chromatography-Mass Spectrometry: Comparison to Product Label and to Values Determined by Microbiological Assay.” *Cereal Chemistry* 87:42–49. DOI: 10.1094/CCHEM-87-1-0042. Full paper: https://www.ars.usda.gov/ARSUserFiles/80400525/Articles/CrlChem_87_42-49.pdf.

Twelve popular US products were sampled from retail outlets in 12 statistically selected regions in 2006; four composites per product pooled three locations each. Folic acid was quantified with LC-MS using a 13C-labelled internal standard after trienzyme treatment and solid-phase extraction. Values remain per 100 g as-sold cereal; no dry-matter conversion is made. This is a study-era multi-region sample, not a current brand-specific concentration.
