# Batch 062 — NIST ground turmeric curcumin

## Result
NIST Certificate of Analysis for SRM 3299 reports a certified curcumin mass fraction of **12.17 ± 0.56 mg/g dry mass** in ground *Curcuma longa* rhizome powder (Table 1). The value is arithmetically normalized to 12,170 mg/kg dry mass. The certificate gives the approximate 95% expanded uncertainty and traceability to SI mass fraction. NIST used LC-absorbance and collaborating laboratories used LC-absorbance/fluorescence; value assignment combines NIST results and the collaborating-lab median.

## Decision
One candidate row is recorded as **held / excluded from edible exposure mapping**. The certificate explicitly states SRM 3299 is for research use and not for human consumption. Although it is turmeric rhizome powder and the value is highly authoritative, it is an irradiated reference material rather than an ordinary culinary sample. The result does not increase accepted food coverage.

## Sources
- NIST, Standard Reference Material 3299 Certificate of Analysis: https://tsapps.nist.gov/srmext/certificates/3299.pdf (Table 1, pages 1–3).
- Rimmer et al. 2020, “Determination of Curcuminoids in Turmeric Dietary Supplements by HPLC-DAD: Multi-laboratory Study Through the NIH-ODS/NIST Quality Assurance Program,” DOI 10.1093/jaoacint/qsaa048; https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8174018/.

## Analytical details
NIST certificate describes duplicate 100 mg test portions from each of 10 packets; methanol extraction, end-over-end rotation and ultrasonication; mixed-mode C18-phenyl LC with absorbance at 425 nm. Calibration materials were gravimetrically prepared and their purity assessed. Moisture conversion factor is 0.9146 g dry mass per g as-received material.
