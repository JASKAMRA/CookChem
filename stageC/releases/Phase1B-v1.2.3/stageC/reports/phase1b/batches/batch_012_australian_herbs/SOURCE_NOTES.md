# Batch 012 — Australian-grown culinary herbs

Twenty-five distinct Stage B compounds were screened against Ali et al. (2021), DOI 10.3390/antiox10111770, PMID 34829641. The study screened 84 phenolics across 10 culinary herbs; Table S1 is explicitly titled HPLC-MS semi-quantification.

Fifteen quantitative food records were ingested for seven compounds: rosmarinic acid (six herbs), carnosol, carnosic acid, protocatechuic acid (two herbs), chlorogenic acid, caffeic acid (two herbs), and quercetin (two herbs). The source reports µg/g dry weight and triplicate analyses; the pipeline converts µg/g to mg/kg without changing the numerical value. Semi-quantitative status, dried sample state, and limitations are preserved in every record.

Catechin and epicatechin were not mapped to stereospecific Stage B IDs. Targets not found in the accessible exact-value panel are recorded as source-panel limited, not as absence from food literature. No exposure calculations were made.
