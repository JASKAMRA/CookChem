# Batch 117 — targeted review of the nine Phase 1B gaps

## Accepted

Added ginkgoneolic acid (ginkgolic acid C13:0), Stage B V20 compound `MOL-V13-CID-161306`, from a primary HPLC study of processed Ginkgo biloba leaf tea. Table 4 reports the dark tea (DT) product at **861.11 ± 1.90 µg/g dry tea (n=3)**. The registered numerical concentration is the mean (861.11 µg/g = 861.11 mg/kg); the SD and replicate count are retained in the record notes. This is dried tea product, not brewed infusion. The source describes Ginkgo leaf tea products; it is curated as a food product and no supplement exposure is inferred. Source: Li et al., *J Sci Food Agric* (2023), DOI [10.1002/jsfa.12577](https://doi.org/10.1002/jsfa.12577), PMID [36973882](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36973882/).

- **7-Methoxyflavanone:** no compound-specific quantitative ordinary-food result verified.

No value inferred from abstracts or unrelated matrices. [Primary paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfca.2011.10.006).
- **5,4′-Dihydroxy-3,6,7,8,3′-pentamethoxyflavone:** no target-specific quantitative ordinary-food value verified.
- **Eupatorin:** located Java-tea values are extract-based (mg/g extract), not raw leaf or beverage concentrations; not accepted as food composition. [Primary study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5980324/).
- **Dodecyl gallate and octyl gallate:** located bakery analytical-method paper establishes a method, but its accessible abstract provides no positive sample-level concentrations; spiking/recovery data are not food measurements. [Primary method paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0021-9673(98)00601-3).
- **Prunin:** quantitative dormant peach-bud work is not an ordinary edible peach-food measurement; recent profiling leads did not yield an accepted calibrated food concentration. [Primary bud study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC396089/).
