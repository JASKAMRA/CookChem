# Batch 057 — phloretin in apple peel and flesh

## Source

Zielińska D. “Electroactive Phenolic Contributors and Antioxidant Capacity of Flesh and Peel of 11 Apple Cultivars Measured by Cyclic Voltammetry and HPLC–DAD–MS/MS.” *Antioxidants*. 2020;9(11):1054. DOI: 10.3390/antiox9111054; PMID: 33126563. Primary full text: https://www.mdpi.com/2076-3921/9/11/1054

## Screen outcome

The study measured phloretin separately in peel and flesh from 11 ripe *Malus domestica* cultivars harvested in Poland in 2019. The source lists a phloretin authentic standard, identifies phloretin with HPLC-ESI-MS/MS (MRM 273.1→227.1/166.8/123.1), quantifies using HPLC-DAD and external-standard calibration, and reports Table 3 means ± SD on a fresh-weight basis. Twenty-two candidate rows retain each cultivar/tissue mean; µg/g FW is numerically equal to mg/kg FW. Phloridzin is tabulated separately and was not conflated with the aglycone.

- Peel, Quinte through Ligol: 0.96±0.02, 0.91±0.08, 0.85±0.02, 0.64±0.01, 1.38±0.08, 1.32±0.02, 0.73±0.03, 2.25±0.05, 1.24±0.05, 1.2±0.01, 0.66±0.04 µg/g FW.
- Flesh, Quinte through Ligol: 0.72±0.03, 0.42±0.01, 0.72±0.01, 0.51±0.02, 0.6±0.03, 0.41±0.0, 0.47±0.01, 0.75±0.03, 0.65±0.03, 1.38±0.08, 0.43±0.01 µg/g FW.

## Review caveat

Methods state that ten fruits were randomly selected, pooled separately by peel/flesh, then freeze-dried; moisture was measured and Table 3 reports FW. Its table note gives n=4 mean ± SD, but whether those four values represent independent biological samples or technical replicates needs source review. Candidates do not alter accepted coverage until review and checksum-gated ingestion.
