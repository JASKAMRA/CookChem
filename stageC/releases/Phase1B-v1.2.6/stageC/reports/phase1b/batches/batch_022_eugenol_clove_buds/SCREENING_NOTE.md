# Batch 022 — eugenol in clove buds: range only

## Screening decision

A validated primary food-matrix method measured eugenol in two clove-bud samples, reporting a range of 2.12 × 10³–55.5 × 10³ mg/kg. The exact per-sample values are not available in the accessible Table 3 summary. No point-valued candidate rows are created because splitting the range into invented sample values or treating endpoints as means would be invalid. The metadata discrepancy is an additional provenance hold.

## Source

Luu Thi Huyen Trang et al. (2023). “A rapid method for the determination of alkenylbenzenes in spices by gas chromatography - high resolution mass spectrometry (GC-HRMS).” *Vietnam Journal of Food Control*, 6(4), 380–392. The article landing page reports DOI `10.47866/2615-9252/vjfc.4159`, while the accessible PDF header reports DOI `10.47866/2615-9252/vjfc.4162`; the issue contents page also lists a different page range for this title. Bibliographic identity needs publisher clarification. Full text/PDF: https://vjfc.nifc.gov.vn/ajax/research/getfile?filecode=36165d9c-8195-4fc6-8100-942278798cbc. The study analyzed two *Syzygium aromaticum* clove-bud samples collected in Hanoi. It used ultrasonic extraction and validated GC-HRMS; the abstract reports LOD 0.05 mg/kg, LOQ 0.15 mg/kg, recoveries 90.1–107%, and RSD 1–6.6% across analytes.
