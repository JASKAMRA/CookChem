# Batch 111 — all-trans-β-carotene in commercial sweet potato

## Decision

Five quantitative observations are accepted in the isolated StageB_V20 successor probe for all-trans-β-carotene from five commercial sweet-potato cultivars. The source reports trans-β-carotene in Table 5 on a dry-weight basis, with means ± SD (n=3 roots per cultivar): Beauregard 33.99 ± 2.54, Bellevue 48.19 ± 5.96, Murasaki-29 0.31 ± 0.11, New Orleans 42.96 ± 9.80, and WSPF 0.47 ± 0.26 mg/100 g DW. The source states that all-trans-β-carotene was the main carotenoid and that no cis β-carotene isomers were detected.

The study used HPLC-DAD, a β-carotene reference standard, external calibration (R²=0.999), and reports the observed matrix as commercial sweet-potato root flesh. Samples were collected from commercial farms in Queensland, Australia; dry-matter normalization is retained exactly and is not converted to fresh weight. The Stage B V20 exact all-trans-β-carotene identity was matched by the connector.
