# Batch 029 — sulforaphane in Beneforté broccoli (analytical hydrolysis basis)

Abukhabta et al., *European Journal of Nutrition* (published online 2020; issue 2021), DOI 10.1007/s00394-020-02322-0, PMID 32651764. The primary study reports raw broccoli at 0.57 µmol/g dry weight and cooked broccoli (100 °C, 12 min) at 0.58 µmol/g dry weight. It used glucoraphanin-rich Beneforté broccoli. Sample powder was lyophilized; sulforaphane extraction used 0.5 g powder in water for 1 h at 30 °C to ensure myrosinase hydrolysis, followed by dichloromethane extraction and GC–MS quantified against external sulforaphane standard.

Conversions use MW 177.29 g/mol: µmol/g × 177.29 = mg/kg. Records remain dry-weight values. These represent sulforaphane generated/recovered after analytical hydrolysis, not directly measured pre-existing free sulforaphane in intact food. We do not infer fresh-weight concentrations, replicate n, or convert precursor glucoraphanin into sulforaphane records. Candidates require a target exposure-basis review before acceptance.
