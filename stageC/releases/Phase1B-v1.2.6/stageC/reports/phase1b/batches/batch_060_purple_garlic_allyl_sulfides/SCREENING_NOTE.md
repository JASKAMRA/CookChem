# Batch 060 — purple garlic diallyl sulfides

## Source

“HS-SPME/GC–MS and E-nose-based characterization of volatile compounds in purple garlic across eight Chinese regions.” Primary article: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12363586/ (Table 2; methods §§2.2–2.3).

## Screen outcome

Prepared 16 sample-level candidates: diallyl sulfide (8 positive cells), diallyl disulfide (6), and diallyl trisulfide (2). Table 2 means ± SD are in µg/g; each is the mean of triplicate biological samples. Eight sample codes are retained as printed: DLDS, GSML, HNZM, LNZG, QHLD, SCNJ, SCWJ and SDJX. The source caption expands regions; its table uses LNZG while the caption uses LNGZ, a spelling discrepancy preserved here. “–” means not detected and was omitted, never converted to zero.

## Identity and quantitation limits

Table 2 lists exact target names, formulas and CAS numbers, but identity assignment is based on NIST 14 library comparison with match index >60%; no compound-specific authentic standards or response-factor calibration are reported. Concentrations use target/internal-standard peak-area ratio multiplied by the mass ratio, with 2-octanol as internal standard. These are internal-standard estimates, not demonstrated analyte-specific calibrated concentrations. Matrix is fresh edible garlic clove homogenate, stored at −80 °C.

Candidates are held for analytical identity/calibration review and the active Stage B checksum gate; accepted coverage does not change.


## Stronger-method comparator located (Batch 061)

A 2021 raw-garlic-paste HPLC study reports compound-standard quantified allicin, diallyl disulfide and diallyl trisulfide on a dry-weight basis (Batch 061). This is a better calibrated comparator for these target identities, although its garlic paste includes added water and the dry-weight denominator still needs review. Batch 060 remains a distinct fresh purple-garlic cohort and its sample-code means remain held due to library-match identity and semiquantitation limitations.
