# Batch 119 — final seven gap screen

**Scope:** the seven identity-complete compounds still uncovered after Phase1B-v1.2.4. This is a source adjudication pass; no food-composition rows are added.

## Decisions

| Compound | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Oroxylin A | Hold | Edible young fruit is reported as non-detect; numeric positive values are for extracts/mature pod coats and do not represent ordinary edible-food concentration. Isolation yield is not native concentration. |
| 7-Methoxyflavanone | Hold | No exact analyte plus calibrated ordinary-food value verified. Similar methoxyflavanones are not interchangeable. |
| Amentoflavone | Hold | Primary HPLC-PDA study covers edible Garcinia fruits and authentic standards, but accessible material did not expose numeric sample values; medicinal material values do not qualify. |
| 5,4′-Dihydroxy-3,6,7,8,3′-pentamethoxyflavone | Hold | Citrus papers often report positional isomers. No verified exact CID 100625 isomer with numeric food concentration. |
| Dodecyl gallate | Hold | No native positive food measurement found; spikes, regulatory limits, and additive-use experiments are ineligible. |
| Octyl gallate | Hold | Same: method recovery spikes and additive/process studies do not establish native food concentration. |
| Prunin | Hold candidate | Commercial wines report 0.02–0.10 mg/L with ND in two sample rows. The authors quantify flavanones using naringenin as the proxy standard, so this is not accepted as exact-analyte quantitative data. A 2021 apple-pulp source with authentic Prunin calibration remains a lead, but its numeric Table S1 values were not retrievable. |

## Coverage outcome

No new accepted compounds. Coverage remains **123/130 identity-complete compounds**, with **7 uncovered**; food records and mappings remain **582/582**. The 130 minimum is still unmet. No Stage B or Phase1 baseline data were changed.
