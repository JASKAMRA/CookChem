# Batch 099 — final re-review of the 10 uncovered targets

## Decision

No new records were promoted. The final searches found either out-of-scope medicinal/supplement matrices, qualitative occurrence without a traceable numeric result for the exact Stage B analyte, or numeric values that could not be independently verified in the primary source with an eligible food matrix. The 10 targets therefore remain gaps under the existing Phase 1B screening rules.

## Findings by target

- **Baicalein, baicalin, wogonin:** quantitative studies include *Scutellaria baicalensis* medicinal root, commercial root tinctures, and a roasted Huangqin medicinal herbal tea study. The root-tincture paper explicitly describes medicinal products; Phase 1B rule 6 excludes them. Values were not transferred to ordinary foods.
- **Scutellarein, oroxylin A, 7-methoxyflavanone, wogonoside:** final searches returned botanical occurrence/structure reports and *Scutellaria* root phytochemistry. No eligible ordinary-food quantitative value for the exact Stage B target was established. Isolated compounds or neighboring structures were not treated as food measurements.
- **Amentoflavone:** final search did not locate a traceable quantitative value in an eligible ordinary-food matrix; retrieved contexts were botanical/supplement-oriented.
- **Irilone:** the primary Tsao et al. (2006) abstract confirms HPLC-DAD-MS profiling of 13 red-clover cultivars and includes irilone among minor aglycones. A later herbal monograph reproduces a single dry-leaf value (0.06 mg/g), but the primary table value and its sample/statistical meaning could not be independently checked in accessible full text. Other numeric leads concern supplements/clinical extracts. The secondary number remains held rather than presented as verified food composition.
- **5,4′-Dihydroxy-3,6,7,8,3′-pentamethoxyflavone:** citrus-flavonoid reviews catalog related/occurrence reports, but no primary numeric concentration for this exact target in an eligible edible citrus matrix was verified. Similar PMFs were not substituted.

No measurements or records were invented or transferred between products/species. A future scope revision could admit medicinal botanical foods as a separate matrix class, but this pass kept the existing Phase 1B rule 6 unchanged.
