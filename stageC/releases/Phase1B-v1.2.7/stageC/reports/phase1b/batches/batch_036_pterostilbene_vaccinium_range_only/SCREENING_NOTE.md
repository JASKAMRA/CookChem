# Batch 036 — Pterostilbene in Vaccinium berries (range-only)

## Screening decision

Two primary studies confirm food occurrence and report only study-level ranges in the accessible records. Rimando et al. (2004) found pterostilbene in two V. ashei cultivars and V. stamineum at 99–520 ng/g dry sample. Rimando & Barney (2005) reported 0.12–2.74 µg/g lyophilized berry across 17 berry samples representing cultivars and nine Vaccinium species. These cohorts and units/bases are not pooled. Neither range identifies the per-cultivar/sample values needed for candidate food records, so no point rows were prepared.

## Sources

Rimando AM, Kalt W, Magee JB, Dewey J, Ballington JR. “Resveratrol, pterostilbene, and piceatannol in Vaccinium berries.” *J Agric Food Chem.* 2004;52(15):4713–4719. DOI: 10.1021/jf040095e; PMID: 15264904. https://doi.org/10.1021/jf040095e.

Rimando AM, Barney DL. “Resveratrol and naturally occurring analogues in Vaccinium species.” *Acta Horticulturae* 680 (2005). DOI: 10.17660/ActaHortic.2005.680.20. https://doi.org/10.17660/ActaHortic.2005.680.20.
