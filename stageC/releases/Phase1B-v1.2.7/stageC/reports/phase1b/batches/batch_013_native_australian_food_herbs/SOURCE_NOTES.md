# Batch 013 — native Australian dried herbs and fruits

Twenty-five distinct Stage B targets were screened against Ali et al. (2023), DOI 10.3390/plants12050993, PMID 36903854. The study used dried bush mint, river mint, bush tomatoes and sea parsley, with triplicate extraction, and labels Table S2 metabolite results semi-quantitative.

Twenty-one records were accepted for nine exact analytes: rosmarinic acid, chlorogenic acid, caffeic acid, diosmetin, acacetin, luteolin, diosmin, kaempferol and resveratrol. Values preserve µg/g dried-sample basis and mean ± SD provenance. No aglycone/glycoside or stereoisomer substitution was made.

Gallic acid, protocatechuic acid, carnosol and carnosic acid have source-confirmed quantitative evidence or analyte presence, but the exact matrix-specific Table S2 values were not extracted in this run, so they remain pending in `CANDIDATES.csv`. No exposures were calculated.
