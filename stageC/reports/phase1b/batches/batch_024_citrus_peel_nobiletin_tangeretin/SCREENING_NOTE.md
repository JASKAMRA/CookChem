# Batch 024 — citrus-peel nobiletin and tangeretin

## Disposition

Prepared 50 sample-level candidates (25 samples × 2 analytes). These remain candidates; accepted coverage is unchanged because ingestion/mapping is blocked by the Stage B integrity gate.

## Evidence

Luo et al. (2018), *Food Science & Nutrition*, DOI: 10.1002/fsn3.569, PMID: 29564108. Full text: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5849905/ . Table 6 reports nobiletin and tangeretin separately in mg/g for 25 citrus-peel samples, with triplicate mean ± SD. The paper names both reference standards, reports their structural identification (EI-MS, 1H NMR, 13C NMR), describes 98% purity, and gives calibration curves. Fruit was collected fresh; peel was removed and sun-dried 5–7 days before analysis. Powdered peel (0.5 g) was extracted with methanol and analyzed by HPLC-UV at 330 nm.

Reported mg/g values were converted to mg/kg dry peel (×1000). No fresh-weight conversion was made. Table 1 supplies cultivar and geographic details; sample IDs are preserved. S11 cultivar text is not clear, so it is recorded as unspecified. Replicate injections/assays (n=3) are not three independent food lots.
