# Phase 2 Scientific Limitations

## Cooked concentrations

There are **zero** valid Phase1B cooked-concentration outputs. A numerical answer is blocked where source measurement bases, food states, matrix/context, or retention conditions do not match. This is an evidence limitation, not a failed arithmetic operation.

## Turmeric and pepper

- Ten Phase1B turmeric commercial-powder measurements × two Suresh contexts produce 20 candidate combinations. The powder paper gives no explicit moisture value or conversion. The cooking study lyophilizes cooked turmeric samples and reports mg/g spice without a denominator bridge. Do not assume equal water content or apply generic moisture.
- Black-pepper piperine uses a retail sample aggregate reported as 4418 ± 946 mg/100 g (44,180 mg/kg), with no moisture basis. The source says it was homogenized but does not establish whether the retail item was purchased whole or ground. Suresh powder scenarios are boiling 10 min, boiling 20 min, and pressure cook 10 min at 15 psi. No scenario is applied to the retail value.
- Tamarind is an explicit separate turmeric study context; it is not pooled with turmeric alone, and both are ingredient-context records.

## EGCG and other observed analytes

The Stage B EGCG tea evidence is qualitative brewing/extraction/epimerization evidence, not a single numerical retention factor. The 117 source observations contain 24 analytes, but most do not have both a linked Phase1B ordinary-food concentration and a Stage B quantitative compatible retention record. Pumpkin-leaf analytes are not present as matched pumpkin-leaf Phase1B composition records in Phase1B-v1.3.0. These observations therefore remain direct research evidence only.

## Apparent ratio limits

The 117 pair dataset has condition-level group means, not matched replicate-level pairs. The pumpkin-leaf paper calculates on a dry-weight basis and discards cooking media; without edible cooked yield and analyte in discarded media, concentration ratios are not total mass retention. Turmeric cooked mass yield is not reported. Ratio uncertainty cannot be reconstructed without raw replicate values/covariance. Ratios above 100% are retained as reported concentration increases, not treated as impossible mass gains.

## Identity and model limits

Only 19 strict Stage A/B identity links and one manual Curcumin link are accepted. Of 45 candidates, 27 are possible, 9 conflicts, and 9 unresolved. CID equality does not replace source-level structure verification. Model inference remains blocked because the exact model training snapshot hash is absent; model package versions alone cannot repair that mismatch.
