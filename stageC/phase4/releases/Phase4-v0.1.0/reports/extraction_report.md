The source is White JR et al., *Clinical Toxicology* 2016;54(4):308–312. DOI 10.3109/15563650.2016.1146740; PMID 27100333. Primary source record: <https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27100333/>. Full-text link listed by PubMed: <https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4898153/>. The extracted location is PubMed abstract Materials, Results, Discussion, and Conclusion.

The abstract states that 24 healthy subjects aged 18–30 completed five 160 mg caffeine conditions and that 10 serial plasma samples were collected after each condition. It describes similar mean concentration-time profiles with overlapping standard deviations; although omnibus tests found differences in mean Cmax and Vdss/F, pairwise comparisons were not statistically significant. The record preserves this nuance and does not infer a magnitude or equivalence. No numerical PK estimate, uncertainty interval, or underlying individual data were extracted. It therefore cannot be used as a numerical modifier for food concentration or recipe exposure.

The abstract-level conclusion is not independently accepted. Full text review, independent extraction verification, compound identity adjudication, and matrix/population applicability review remain pending.
