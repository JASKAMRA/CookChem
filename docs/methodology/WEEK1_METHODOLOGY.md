# Week 1 Methodology

Week 1 reviewed six papers, two per team member. The review established that retention depends on food, compound, cooking method, and cooking conditions. Measurement and assay methodology matter for comparing literature-derived retention values. Retention greater than 100% should not automatically be treated as an error because apparent increases may reflect extractability, chemical transformation, or changes in composition/weight. Molecular descriptors are motivated as future predictive features, but must be generated reproducibly from validated structures.
