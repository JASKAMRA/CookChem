# Future Research Workflow

This is a proposed sequence, not completed work or a replacement for official project task registers.

## Gate 0 — scientific owner designates an analysis dataset

Choose one ACR snapshot (Batch021 or verified reconstruction, or a documented successor) and record exact-byte hashes for data, source ledger, code, feature contract, target formula, and split plan. Reconcile the 439-row manifest mismatch before reusing its results. Keep `Retention_Final` outside the ACR dataset family.

**Exit evidence:** signed owner designation and a version/hash-locked dataset manifest.

## Gate 1 — evidence and endpoint audit

Sample-check source locators and raw/cooked pair values against primary tables; verify compound identity, food state, unit/basis, cooking conditions, replicate handling, and ACR arithmetic. Keep unresolved records explicitly pending. Do not infer true retention without mass/yield data.

**Exit evidence:** record-level audit trail, inclusion/exclusion decisions, and validated data dictionary.

## Gate 2 — prespecified exploratory re-analysis

Only after the approved environment and frozen snapshot are available, reproduce existing EDA/model comparisons in a scratch output directory. Do not overwrite historical outputs. Confirm fold assignments, preprocessing fit scope, prediction alignment, and metrics. Report all group counts and negative R².

**Exit evidence:** exact command, dependency lock, hashes, regenerated outputs, and discrepancy report.

## Gate 3 — independent evidence acquisition

Prioritize primary studies that repeat the same compound × matrix × cooking method × basis × protocol cell across independent research sources. Current six categorical overlaps are reported as zero strict protocol-identical replications. Record measurement basis and conditions at the source and preserve separate lots/experimental units.

**Exit evidence:** qualified, source-linked replicated cells and a prespecified inclusion rule.

## Gate 4 — confirmatory generalization evaluation

Pre-register the locked case set and primary endpoint. Hold out whole DOI/lab families that provide compatible data and avoid tuning on the confirmatory set. Compare simple baselines and selected models using identical outer splits. Include uncertainty/fold stability and error analysis by groups with adequate support.

**Exit evidence:** independent source-heldout evaluation with predeclared metrics and uncertainty reporting. This is not available yet.

## Gate 5 — evaluate a distinct mass-retention endpoint if data support it

Acquire compatible raw/cooked food mass or measured yield, moisture/basis conversion data, portion definitions, and cooking-liquid handling. Define an equation and unit contract before calculation. Keep ACR and total-mass retention in separate targets/tables.

**Exit evidence:** compatible inputs, documented calculation trace, and independent scientific review.

## Go/no-go rules

- Do not add model complexity to compensate for absent source overlap.
- Do not label a convenience split as independent validation.
- Do not interpret feature importance causally.
- Do not create deployment or consumer advice from experimental ACR metrics.
- Stop or narrow the research question when evidence compatibility fails.
