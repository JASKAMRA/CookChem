# Scientific dataset expansion — controlled batch 012

The controlled master becomes **749 verified observations from 25 DOI families**. Batch 012 adds 30 primary-table pairs from Narwojsz et al. (2025), DOI `10.3390/app15010254`, and preserves all 719 parent rows.

Tables 3–4 directly report individual UHPLC-DAD-MS phenolics and RP-HPLC-DAD carotenoids in whole unpeeled carrot roots. The six compatible established identities span boiling, steel-pot steaming, combi-oven steaming, baking and sous-vide. All values remain µg/g dry weight. No zeros, technical replicates or aggregate endpoints are used, and absent exact identities are not inferred. No model is retrained; 251 observations remain to the 1,000 target.
