# v0.5 source admission

## Admitted

Narwojsz et al. (2020), DOI `10.1007/s11130-020-00808-0`, Table 2 reports HPLC vitamin C in compatible mg/100 g DW for raw Lord-cultivar potato and five treatments. Methods state: boiling 35 min (5:1 water:raw), steaming 50 min, 103 °C combi steaming 40 min, 750 W microwave 7 min, and 250 °C grilling 45 min. Five apparent-ratio rows are admitted; missing temperatures are deliberately blank.

## Excluded/pending

The Frontiers phenolic study (DOI `10.3389/fnut.2020.580550`) gives mg/kg but does not state fresh/dry basis in the table; it is excluded. Lee et al. (DOI `10.1007/s10068-017-0281-1`) remains pending primary-table retrieval.
