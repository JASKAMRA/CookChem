# Candidate B extraction

Musilova et al. (2020), DOI `10.1155/2020/8856260`, Table 6 reports HPLC-DAD chlorogenic, neochlorogenic, and trans-ferulic acid in mg/kg FW. Methods: 3-mm peeled sweet-potato slices; boiling 10 min, steaming 15 min at 97 ± 2 C, microwave 5 min/800 W, baking 15 min/200 C. Four cultivar-origin strata, quadruplicate means.

Admitted: 32 directly comparable apparent-ratio pairs for chlorogenic and trans-ferulic acids. Neochlorogenic acid: excluded from model-ready data because no verified canonical molecular descriptor record exists in the established schema; values are not discarded from the audit.
