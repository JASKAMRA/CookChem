# Source-confounding diagnostic

There are 10 DOI families. The accompanying NMI table measures association with DOI family, not causal influence. High association indicates that a held-out source can represent a different compound-food-protocol domain rather than an independent replication. The parent v0.6 controlled analysis already found negative source-LOSO performance; this iteration intentionally does not retrain on the same structurally sparse evidence.

Only **6** categorical compound × food × method × basis cells span more than one DOI family, and **0** satisfy this audit's stricter fully reported, identical-protocol criterion. This is the central overlap limitation.
