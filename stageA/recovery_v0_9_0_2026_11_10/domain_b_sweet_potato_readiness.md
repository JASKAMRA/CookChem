# Domain B — phenolic acids in sweet potato

This scope contains **32 observations** from **1 DOI family**, 4 cultivar-origin matrices, 4 methods and stated fresh-weight basis. Both included compounds have finite recorded descriptors. The design is useful within-source factorial evidence, but cultivar/origin strata are not independent papers or laboratories.

**Decision: C — more matched evidence required.** It is not an honest predictive domain until an independent study supplies comparable raw/cooked fresh-weight chlorogenic and/or ferulic acid measurements with explicit cooking conditions. No model is trained.
