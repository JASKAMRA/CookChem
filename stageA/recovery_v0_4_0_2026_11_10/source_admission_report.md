# v0.4 source admission

## Admitted independent source

Hossain et al. (2023), *Effect of cooking methods on the nutritional quality of selected vegetables at Sylhet City*, DOI `10.1016/j.heliyon.2023.e21709`, reports Table 3 means for beta-carotene and ascorbic acid in mg/100 g FW and states FW in the caption. Table 1 reports 100 g sample, boiling 6 min/150 mL water, steaming 8 min, and 900 W microwave 1 min/6 mL water. This adds 36 experiment-level apparent-ratio observations: 6 matrices × 2 compounds × 3 methods. Temperature is not reported and remains missing.

## Excluded candidate

Ombra et al. (2016), DOI `10.1155/2016/1398298`, was inspected at Table 4. Although raw/cooked pairs and a pressure-cooker protocol are reported, the table caption does not state a raw/cooked common dry- or fresh-weight basis, while rehydration/cooking alters mass. Its 26 historical ratios remain excluded rather than being silently treated as compatible concentration data.
