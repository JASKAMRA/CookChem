# Molecular descriptor coverage

The v0.8 parent has **439 observations**, **49 compound labels**, and **46 canonical molecular identifiers**. All eight recorded RDKit descriptor fields are finite for **439 / 439 (100.0%)** observations. The compound/identifier mapping and source-level completeness are in the accompanying CSVs. This audit does not create structures or impute descriptor values. The historical exclusion of neochlorogenic acid remains outside this dataset and therefore outside this completeness percentage.

Descriptor-complete representation is not evidence of experimental comparability; descriptor availability cannot repair source/protocol confounding.
