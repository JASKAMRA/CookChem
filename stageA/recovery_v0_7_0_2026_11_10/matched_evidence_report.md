# Matched experimental evidence report

- v0.7 has 407 observations from 9 DOI families.
- Lee et al. (2018), DOI `10.1007/s10068-017-0281-1`, adds 28 vitamin-C apparent-concentration pairs on an explicitly stated fresh-weight basis.
- Exact compound × food × method × basis cells represented in at least two DOI families: 6. These are listed in `multi_source_exact_cells.csv`; they are chiefly Vitamin C in carrot and spinach under boiling, steaming, and microwave treatments.
- Exact cells are replication opportunities, but each has only two source observations; they are insufficient for a standalone predictive model or quantitative source-transfer claim.
- Candidate B (`10.1155/2020/8856260`) was verified as a primary open-access article with explicit protocols, but individual phenolic-acid table extraction remains pending; no values were admitted from it.

Decision: **D — continue data acquisition**. The new matched cells improve overlap, but not enough to identify source effects separately from study protocol.
