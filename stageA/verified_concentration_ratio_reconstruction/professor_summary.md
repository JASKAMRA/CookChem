# Professor summary

This reconstruction keeps a single scientifically explicit target: apparent concentration ratio, `100 × C_cooked / C_raw`. It does not claim total-mass retention.

Of 1,124 historical rows, 338 can be re-verified from preserved Batch021 primary-source mappings using exact record ID, DOI, and molecule agreement. No historical targets were silently corrected. The remaining 786 lack an admissible preserved primary-source mapping or have unresolved target semantics/DOI evidence and are excluded from the historical-verified subset.

The reconstructed modelling master has 1016 primary-source-verified observations from 32 DOI families and 48 canonical molecular identities. It includes 678 recovery-labelled primary-source observations beyond the historically mapped subset.

The historical molecular-CV mean fold R² benchmark was 0.4004. The new fixed-configuration molecular-CV mean fold R² is 0.0835; its source-held-out mean fold R² is -0.2535. These describe a cleaner, explicit target and do not prove transferability to unseen sources.

Strongest defensible conclusion: the available evidence supports an apparent-concentration-ratio dataset with traceable raw/cooked pairs. Performance must be judged primarily with source-held-out results, and neither a positive molecular-grouped score nor ratios above 100% establish nutrient mass retention.
